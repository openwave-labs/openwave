## Objective
Address the M4.9 assumptions requested by the reviewer: the pair
potential exponent $n = 1$ and the axial compression factor $q = 1$.
The notebook shows symbolically that $q = 1$ follows from spherical
symmetry at $m = 1$, and runs a three-sector consistency test showing
that $n = 1$ is the only value compatible with the rest of the model.

## Result
$q = 1$ has moved from assumed to derived. $n = 1$ is supported by
three independent sectors (strain equality, force law, metric profile);
every other value breaks at least one. The M4.9 assumption list shrinks
from $\{n = 1, q = 1\}$ to $\{n = 1\}$.
