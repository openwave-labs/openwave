# M4.17 — Local Medium / Field Parameter Dependence $G(B)$ and $\alpha_G(B)$

## Status

DONE (post-hoc)

## Criterion

`Local medium / field parameter dependence` — effective coupling $G(B)$ and $\alpha_G(B)$ vary with local medium/field state while recovering the geometric EWT baseline in the weak-field limit. No `MODELS.md` criterion.

## Objective

Formulate a parametric medium/field-dependent gravitational coupling $G(B)$ and $\alpha_G(B)$ built directly on the geometric baseline $G_{\text{EWT}}$ and $\alpha_G^{\text{geom}}$, parameterized by the normalized local medium/field state $B / B_0$ without altering intrinsic electron constants.

## Outcome

The parametric model reproduces the geometric baseline $G_{\text{EWT}}$ and $\alpha_G^{\text{geom}}$ in the macroscopic/weak-field limit ($B / B_0 \to 0$), by construction ($f_G(0) = 1$), and exhibits smooth suppression across the transition scale $B / B_0 \sim 1$ via a phenomenological saturation ansatz $f_G(B/B_0)$. Numbers, limiting checks, and regime evaluations are in the findings note.
