# OPH theorem and observable inventory

This inventory is pinned to public RER `main` at [`bb1de059`](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/tree/bb1de05927d8c6bb1285182cc91e97410092ec8a).
The [status site](https://status.floatingpragma.ai/) supplies the discovery index;
the claim statements, complete finite theorem stacks, proofs and source receipts at this commit govern the scope.

**OPH develops the consequences of explicit observer constructions down to measurement, records, spatial response, clocks, gauge structure and conditional gravitational reconstruction.**
Architecture and constitutive principles are stated inputs. Their consequences are proved or computed on named domains.
A physical interpretation additionally identifies those same preparations, operations, records, units and uncertainties with observations.
Neither a theorem count nor a status-site label assigns an OpenWave cell.

The 94 entries below are connected mathematical and operational statements, with shared premises and overlapping evidence.
They are not independent experimental successes or a count of completed OpenWave rows.
Standard theorems retain their prior attribution: in particular Busch–Gleason, no cloning, Tsirelson, information projection/data processing,
Doplicher–Roberts/Tannaka and Jacobson-type gravitational reconstruction are not claimed as discoveries of OPH.
The OPH contribution to assess is the specified observer/source realization, finite proof, composition or diagnostic.

**Evidence tags:** F = exact finite theorem on the stated objects; X = retained execution/certified computation;
C = conditional continuum or physical interpretation; I = supplied empirical calibration; D = comparison or diagnostic.
F does not mean every premise is source-selected. X reports the linked source record, not a fresh execution by this inventory.
A Lean link identifies source formalization; this documentation update does not certify a fresh whole-library build.
