# M10.2: one signed source for three static tests

Roadmap: [M10.2](../m10_roadmap.md). Author: Bernhard Mueller.
Candidate: `A-static-signed-source-v1`. Criteria: rows 1, 2 and 7.

## Plan and provenance

This task reproduces an existing author-side result and submits its physical
interpretation for review. It is not a blind prediction or a claim that the
platform registered the original experiment before it ran. The original gates,
inputs, complete records and amendments are retained in the author package.
The reproduction gates below are committed before the platform replay.

The candidate has a 240-position graph with 630 seams and a declared positive
coherent memory law. Its cell-addressed experiment uses 30 seams. The full
630-seam transfer alone defines its energy. The observer prepares and reads the
same signed-coordinate register; fresh preparations serve incompatible assays.

The scalar endpoint law, complete instrument, preparation/read interface and
source join are inspected together. The positive coherent body law is a
primitive of this candidate, not a claim that passive repair implements every
quantum branch. The finite source/charge/energy/field/frame dictionary is the
author's proposal for the three static criteria.

## Transport amendment

The original export pins one interpreter-dependent `.pyc` cache. Its Python
source is retained and hash-verified. The public package omits the cache and
documents its original path/hash in `PORTABILITY.json`. Its replay adapter
removes only that cache pin from a temporary copy of `PREREG.json`; it leaves
the original archive, every scientific input, all thresholds and expected
results unchanged. The archive is verified before and after execution.

## Review and completion

The author supplies the code, compute, run receipts and second-agent audit.
The audit is an independent implementation within the producing group, not
external-group confirmation. Maintainer review determines source admissibility
and each cell's interpretation under the existing platform rules. This task
creates no maintainer obligation to derive a missing construction, rerun a
large computation, or accept a new archival freeze convention.

The submission includes the pinned author package, a small platform replay
script, a method note with equation-to-code links, run receipt, and the audit.
The candidate change and the previous source's failed coherent join are
explicit. Physical dictionaries are proposed, and all shared matrix cells
retain their recorded status until a maintainer decision.

**Scope**: finite charge, compact transfer-energy and tensor/frame observations
on one declared signed-source instrument; no physical clock, free lifetime,
spin magnitude, exchange statistic, continuous spatial symmetry or other
criterion is established by this task.
