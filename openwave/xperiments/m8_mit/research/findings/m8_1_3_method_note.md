# M8.1.3 method note: the small-amplitude data of the Standing-Wave Branches, verified blind

The blind-run record for [M8.1.3](../tasks/m8_1_3_task_details.md), filed and accepted at [#731](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/pull/731) and run on the merged text. The subject is *Nonlinear Standing-Wave Branches on the Poincaré Homology Sphere*, Zenodo version DOI [10.5281/zenodo.24224845](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24224845). This note states the equations first, maps each to the code that computed it, and sets the returns against the frozen claims file. It follows [`dev_docs/METHOD_NOTE.md`](../../../../../dev_docs/METHOD_NOTE.md).

## 1. Equations first

### 1.1 The object

`Γ₀ ⊂ SU(2)` is the group generated by `q₁ = (1 + i + j + k)/2` and `q₂ = (φ + φ⁻¹i + j)/2`, acting on `S³ = SU(2)` by right translation, and `X = S³/Γ₀`. A slot `ρ` is the flat bundle whose sections satisfy `ψ(gh) = ψ(g)ρ(h)`. The three slots studied are `R₂` (dimension 2, lowest level 7), `R₄` (3, level 6) and `R₅` (4, level 6). At the lowest level `n = 2j`, with an intertwiner `η` scaled so that `η†η = I`,

```text
φ_w(g)_a = Σ_{m,m'} w_m D^j_{mm'}(g) η_{m'a},   ∫_X |φ_w|² dV = s_N · V · ‖w‖²,   ψ_w = (s_N V)^{-1/2} φ_w,

Q(w) = V ∫_X |ψ_w|⁴ dV / (∫_X |ψ_w|² dV)²,        h(w) = Q(w) ‖w‖⁴,

f_J(w) = ‖[w ⊗ w]_J‖² / ‖w‖⁴,                     Q = Σ_J q_J f_J        (the channel coefficients).
```

Clebsch-Gordan coefficients are Condon-Shortley, time reversal is `(Θu)_m = (−1)^{j−m} conj(u_{−m})`, and the spin-6 ray invariant is `r̂₆(w) = ‖[w ⊗ Θw]₆‖² / ‖w‖⁴`.

### 1.2 The small-amplitude data (Blocks A and B)

At a unit critical point `u`, `∇h(u) = 4h(u)u`:

```text
M̂_u = Hess h(u) − 4h(u),        K_u = T_u(Γu),  k = dim K_u,        N_u = (ℝu ⊕ K_u)^⊥,
signature = inertia of M̂_u on N_u,      r_u = dim rad(σ|K_u),  σ(x, y) = Re⟨x, iy⟩,
Ŝ_u = i M̂_u,    zero multiplicity = algebraic multiplicity of 0 in det(ξ − Ŝ_u),
slow polynomial = that characteristic polynomial with its zero roots removed, in ν = ξ².
```

`Γ` is translation by SU(2) on the free side, the phase, and the slot's fibre group: charge conjugation in `R₄` and `R₅`, and in `R₂` the `Sp(1)` that the quaternionic structure generates with the phase. A cluster's Krein sign is the sign of `M̂_u` on `ker(Ŝ_u² + τ²)`. In `R₂` the four members replace `i` by `J = Θ`, `k = J∘i`, `(i + J)/√2` and `(i + k)/√2`.

### 1.3 Beyond leading order (Block C)

With `λ_L = L(L + 2)`, `N(ψ) = |ψ|²ψ`, and `Π_L` the projection onto level `L`,

```text
R̃₀(y) = −Σ_{L > n} ‖Π_L N(ψ_y)‖² / (λ_L − λ₀),                       (V = R = 1)
γ_u   = κ_u⁻² (Hess R̃₀(u) − 6 R̃₀(u))|_{K'},    K' = ker M̂_u ∩ N_u,   κ_u = h(u),
D_u   = κ_u⁻² (Hess R̃₀(u) − D³h(u)[ẑ]),         M̂_u ẑ = ∇R̃₀(u) on ℝu ⊕ N_u,
T₁    = (c²/4ω₀) i D_u − (c²/2ω₀²) μ₀ + (μ₀²/2ω₀) i,     μ₀ = χ s_g,  χ = c²/(8ω₀ Q(u)),
c_g   = ℓᵀ T₁ r   with ℓᵀ r = 1,   or the eigenvalues of ℓ_aᵀ T₁ r_b for a semisimple group.
```

In `T₁`, each `i` is the complex structure of `E₀` extended complex-linearly, and `μ₀` and `s_g` are scalars (clause 1 of the handout as run). The formula is evaluated, not derived: whether `c_g` is a coefficient of anything is not asked.

### 1.4 The certificates (Block D)

In the 14 real coordinates `x` of the weighted components `p_m = w_m/√((3 + m)!(3 − m)!)`, with the diagonal metric `G` for which `|x|_G = ‖w‖` and the four given symmetry generators `X_i`, the slice system at a start point `x₀` is

```text
∇h(x) − λ G x − Σ_i c_i G X_i x₀ = 0,     |x|²_G = 1,     ⟨G X_i x₀, x − x₀⟩ = 0   (i = 1..4),
```

19 equations in `(x, λ, c)`. Krawczyk's test on the box of radius `10⁻⁶⁰` about the given centre certifies a unique zero in it.

### 1.5 The rigid families (Block E)

Where `V_{n/2}` restricted to `Γ₀` is `ρ` itself, every lowest-level section has constant modulus, `e^{iωT}ψ_v` solves the field equation with `ω²/c² = n(n + 2)/R² + g₄|v|²`, and the linearization of `ψ = e^{iωT}(ψ_v + ζ)` is restricted to `E₀`.

### 5.2 What the audits tried and could not break

- Criticality is exact, not a tolerance. The residual is zero at all 28 tabulated points, and a perturbation of `10⁻²⁰` or `10⁻³⁰` gives a residual of that order.
- Signatures were reproduced by independent decompositions: Descartes counts on exact characteristic polynomials, 60-digit eigenvalues on an explicit basis of `N_u`, and for Block D a Descartes count on the interval polynomial against `cert`'s Gershgorin route.
- Every zero multiplicity reported is an algebraic multiplicity. Audit `a` checked rank sequences of powers of `Ŝ_u` at every B.1 to B.3 point: each kernel dimension is strictly smaller, and every Jordan block at 0 has size at most 2. Audit `b` set the exact characteristic-polynomial multiplicity against the kernel dimension at the five C5 cases, and in Block D read the multiplicity from a certified nonzero coefficient below the structural zeros.
- `cert`'s quartic equals the audit's coefficient by coefficient (263 monomials per slot), and both rooms' `η` reproduce the audit's exact isotypic projector.
- C5's values do not depend on how the eigenvectors are normalized: a random change of basis inside each eigenspace leaves them within `2·10⁻⁵⁴`.
- At `O₁` and `O₂` the words rest on a single positive slow root. Its sign is certified in two independent codes, `cert`'s and audit `b`'s. The criticality of the six outside points is certified in three, `ca`'s, `cert`'s and audit `b`'s.

### 5.4 What the audits could not check

`|Γ_u| = 24` at `R₂`'s line point rests on two independent numerical searches and on the maximality of the octahedral group among finite subgroups of SO(3); neither room built the 24 elements exactly. The rounding of `cert`'s printed 70-digit decimals is not directed rounding. A misrounding would need an endpoint within about `10⁻¹⁵⁰` of a printed decimal, and none is shown either way.
