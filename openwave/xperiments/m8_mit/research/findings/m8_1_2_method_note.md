```text

Theta (antiunitary):  Theta( sum u_m v_m ) = sum (-1)^m conj(u_m) v_(-m)      Theta^2 = +1 on V_3

                      a root r is the point with polar angle 2 arctan|r| and azimuth arg r
```

### 1.2 One transform, three arguments

```text

ray invariant        rhat_K([u]) = ||rho_K(u)||^2 / ||u||^4
```

## 2. The derivations the agents produced (neither was supplied)

Both agents derived the same closed forms by the same classical route, Peter-Weyl plus the
Clebsch-Gordan series plus Schur orthogonality over normalized Haar measure. The solver's
statement, with the auditor's matching form in its stage-1 return:

```text
|psi(g)|^2 = sum_K rho_K(u)^T D^K(g) R_K                         (checked exactly at a rational SU(2) element)
R_K lies in (V_K)^Gamma, so R_K = 0 wherever the invariant space is zero
B = d ||u||^2 / 7,      A = sum_K ||rho_K(u)||^2 ||R_K||^2 / (2K+1)
w_K = 49 ||R_K||^2 / ((2K+1) d^2),      w_0 = 7 for every d,      Q_d = 1 + w_6 rhat_6 at level 6
Ncal(u) = (1/d) sum_K c_K M_K(u),       c_K = Tr(P A(R_K)) = -sqrt(7/(2K+1)) ||R_K||^2
```

Item 19, the exact zero set, was proved by both agents through binary forms: the spin-8
coupling of `u (x) Theta u (x) u` is, up to a nonzero constant, the Jacobian
`J(F_u^2, F_(Theta u))`; the Leibniz rule reduces it to `2 F_u J(F_u, F_(Theta u))`; and the
supplied equal-degree Jacobian criterion then forces `Theta u = lambda u`. Both found this
route in stage 1, with the `L_8` normalization withheld (section 5 records what that means for
A5).

## 5. Adversarial audit record

The auditor committed both stages before it saw the solver's work, then received the solver's
scripts and returns and tried to refute every item. It refuted no value.

### 5.1 The underdetermination the solver half-found

Both agents flagged that the expansion `Q_d = sum_K w_K rhat_K` does not fix `w_K`. The
solver named one direction of the ambiguity (the `rhat_K` sum to 1). The auditor found the
whole of it: the seven quartics `\|\|rho_K\|\|^2` span four dimensions, forced by
`dim End_SU(2)(Sym² V_3) = 4`, so there are three independent relations, each verified as an
exact polynomial identity, and one expansion of `Q_4` with `w_6 = 0` reproduces the same
function. The frozen values stand under the Peter-Weyl reading both agents used, and one
reading makes `w_6` intrinsic: `Q_d = 1 + w_6 rhat_6` as functions, and `rhat_6` is not
constant, so the slope is unique. That last step is the designer's, not an agent's.

### 5.2 Conventions proved rather than assumed

The two agents mapped quaternions into SU(2) differently. The auditor found the conjugating
element exactly and showed it carries one set of projectors onto the other, so every norm,
dimension, character and coefficient agrees, and only the positions of item 20's roots move,
by that rotation. Its other reading differences (item 8's second ray, item 18's second
normalization, the basis for `N_J`) are conventions with the conversions stated.

### 5.4 Provenance, after adjudication

The author's verification package is not in the public repository, at the pinned commit or on
the default branch, so no three-way agreement is stated. Under the roadmap's standing rule such
agreement would have been a provenance statement, not a stronger verification, and its absence
changes no verdict. The paper's text was checked instead: the pinned blob re-hashes to its
recorded SHA-256, and its § 5.2 fixes `w_K` by the Peter-Weyl formula, the reading both agents
used.
