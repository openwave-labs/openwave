## 1. Setup

### 1.1 The group and the slots

Identify $`S^3`$ with $`\mathrm{SU}(2)`$, the unit quaternions $`w + xi + yj + zk`$, with the round metric of radius $`R`$. Let $`\Gamma_0 \subset \mathrm{SU}(2)`$ be the finite subgroup generated by

```math
q_1 \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\left(1 + i + j + k\right) , \qquad q_2 \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\left(\varphi + \varphi^{-1} i + j\right) , \qquad \varphi \;=\; \tfrac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2} .
```

$`\Gamma_0`$ acts on $`S^3`$ by right translation, and $`X = S^3/\Gamma_0`$ is the quotient, of volume $`V = \mathrm{vol}(S^3)/\lvert\Gamma_0\rvert`$. Its representation theory is not supplied; derive what you need from the two generators (item 0(a)).

For an irreducible unitary representation $`\rho`$ of $`\Gamma_0`$ on $`V_\rho`$, the **slot** $`\rho`$ is the flat bundle on $`X`$ whose sections are the maps $`\psi \colon \mathrm{SU}(2) \to \mathbb{C}^{\dim\rho}`$, written as rows, with $`\psi(gh) = \psi(g)\,\rho(h)`$ for $`h \in \Gamma_0`$. The **level** $`\ell`$ is the eigenvalue $`\ell(\ell+2)/R^2`$ of $`-\Delta`$; the sections at level $`\ell`$ are $`V_{\ell/2} \otimes \mathrm{Hom}_{\Gamma_0}(\rho, V_{\ell/2})`$, realized in Section 1.3. Three slots are studied here, named by their dimension and the lowest level at which they occur:

| Name | $`\dim\rho`$ | lowest level $`n`$ | $`j = n/2`$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $`R_2`$ | 2 | 7 | 7/2 |
| $`R_4`$ | 3 | 6 | 3 |
| $`R_5`$ | 4 | 6 | 3 |

These names are labels, not the names of characters in any standard table; item 0(b) asks you to confirm that each is well defined. Block E uses the slots, the trivial representation included, whose restriction of $`V_{n/2}`$ at their lowest level $`n`$ is $`\rho`$ itself; item E.1 asks you to find them.

### 1.2 Spin representations, couplings and time reversal

$`V_j = \mathrm{Sym}^{2j}\mathbb{C}^2`$ is the spin-$`j`$ representation of $`\mathrm{SU}(2)`$, with orthonormal weight basis $`v_m = \lvert j\,m\rangle`$, $`-j \le m \le j`$, and $`D^j(g)`$ its matrix. Clebsch–Gordan coefficients $`\langle j_1 m_1; j_2 m_2 \mid J M\rangle`$ are in the Condon–Shortley convention: real, with $`\langle j\,j; j\,{-j} \mid 2j\,0\rangle > 0`$. For $`a, b \in V_j`$, $`[a \otimes b]_J`$ is the spin-$`J`$ part of $`a \otimes b`$, with components $`\sum_{m_1 + m_2 = M}\langle j\,m_1; j\,m_2 \mid J\,M\rangle\, a_{m_1} b_{m_2}`$.

Time reversal on $`V_j`$ is the antiunitary map

```math
(\Theta u)_m \;=\; (-1)^{\,j-m}\,\overline{u_{-m}} .
```

The spin-$`K`$ operators on $`V_j`$ are $`(T^K_M)_{mm'} = (-1)^{j-m'}\langle j\,m; j\,{-m'} \mid K\,M\rangle`$; they are orthonormal in the Hilbert–Schmidt product. For $`w \in V_j`$, $`(ww^\dagger)_K`$ is the spin-$`K`$ component of the operator $`ww^\dagger`$ under conjugation, $`\sum_M \mathrm{tr}\big((T^K_M)^\dagger ww^\dagger\big)\, T^K_M`$.

**Weighted coordinates.** For $`w = \sum_m w_m v_m \in V_j`$, $`p_m = w_m/\sqrt{(j+m)!\,(j-m)!}`$.

### 1.3 The lowest level of a slot, and the reduced quartic

Let $`\rho`$ be one of $`R_2, R_4, R_5`$, at its lowest level $`n = 2j`$, where it occurs in $`V_j`$ restricted to $`\Gamma_0`$ with multiplicity one (item 0(c)). Choose an intertwiner $`\eta \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\Gamma_0}(\rho, V_j)`$, with $`\rho`$ read off as $`\rho(h) = \eta^\dagger D^j(h)\,\eta`$, so that $`D^j(h)\,\eta = \eta\,\rho(h)`$, and scale it so that $`\eta^\dagger\eta = I_\rho`$. Put

```math
\phi_w(g)_a \;=\; \sum_{m,m'} w_m\, D^j_{mm'}(g)\, \eta_{m'a} , \qquad w \in V_j ,
```

and let $`s`$ be the constant with $`\int_X \lvert\phi_w\rvert^2\, dV = s\,V\lVert w\rVert^2`$ for every $`w`$ (item 0(c)). The lowest-level sections are $`\psi_w = (sV)^{-1/2}\,\phi_w`$, so that $`\int_X \lvert\psi_w\rvert^2\, dV = \lVert w\rVert^2`$: $`w \mapsto \psi_w`$ is an isometry of $`V_j`$, with its Condon–Shortley norm, onto $`E_0`$, the lowest eigenspace, on which $`\mathrm{SU}(2)`$ acts by translation on the free side. On $`w`$ that action is $`D^j`$ or its conjugate, according to the side; the generators of `inputs.json` realize $`D^j`$'s on the weighted coordinates, and no scored quantity but the circle of Block C, fixed there on components, depends on which. $`Q`$ is a ratio and does not see this normalization; $`\widetilde R_0`$ of Block C is of degree six in $`\psi`$ and does.

The **Jensen ratio** and the **reduced quartic** are

```math
Q(w) \;=\; \frac{V\int_X \lvert\psi_w\rvert^4\, dV}{\big(\int_X \lvert\psi_w\rvert^2\, dV\big)^2} , \qquad h(w) \;=\; Q(w)\,\lVert w\rVert^4 .
```

$`Q`$ does not change if the measure on $`X`$ is rescaled. The integrals are over $`X`$; computed on $`\mathrm{SU}(2)`$ with its Riemannian volume measure, each is $`1/\lvert\Gamma_0\rvert`$ of the integral over $`\mathrm{SU}(2)`$, since $`\lvert\psi_w\rvert`$ is a function on $`X`$.

**The channel fractions.** For $`w \ne 0`$, $`f_J(w) = \lVert[w \otimes w]_J\rVert^2/\lVert w\rVert^4`$ for $`J = 2j, 2j-2, \ldots`$; they sum to 1. The **channel coefficients** $`q_J`$ of $`\rho`$ are the numbers with $`Q = \sum_J q_J f_J`$ on all of $`V_j`$.

**Two spin-6 functions on $`V_3`$.** The Hilbert–Schmidt factor $`\lVert(ww^\dagger)_6\rVert^2/\lVert w\rVert^4`$, and the ray invariant $`\widehat r_6(w) = \lVert[w \otimes \Theta w]_6\rVert^2/\lVert w\rVert^4`$.

### 1.4 The stationary problem

The field equation in a slot, with $`c`$ the wave speed and $`g_4 = 1`$, is $`c^{-2}\partial_T^2\psi - \Delta\psi + g_4\lvert\psi\rvert^2\psi = 0`$, with the nonlinearity pointwise; standing waves $`e^{i\omega T}\varphi`$ solve $`(-\Delta - \omega^2/c^2)\varphi + g_4\lvert\varphi\rvert^2\varphi = 0`$. The phase space carries the symplectic form $`\sigma(x, y) = \mathrm{Re}\langle x, iy\rangle`$ on $`E_0`$, with $`E_0`$ read as a real space with the inner product $`\mathrm{Re}\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle`$.

**The stationary group $`\Gamma`$** acting on $`E_0`$: translations by $`\mathrm{SU}(2)`$ on the free side; the phase $`w \mapsto e^{i\alpha}w`$; and the fibre group of the slot. In $`R_4`$ and $`R_5`$ that is charge conjugation $`\psi \mapsto \bar\psi C`$, with $`C`$ unitary, $`\bar\rho(h)C = C\rho(h)`$ and $`C\bar C = 1`$. In $`R_2`$, where $`C\bar C = -1`$, $`\psi \mapsto \bar\psi C`$ is a quaternionic structure, and with the phase it generates a group $`\mathrm{Sp}(1)`$ acting on $`E_0`$; item 0(e) asks how it acts on $`E_0`$. Fix $`J = \Theta`$, applied to $`w`$, and $`k \colon w \mapsto J(iw)`$. A complex structure of the family is a unit $`\tau`$ in the real span of $`i`$, $`J`$ and $`k`$; the **four members** read in Block B are those whose complex structure is $`J`$, $`k`$, $`(i + J)/\sqrt{2}`$ and $`(i + k)/\sqrt{2}`$.

## 3. Definitions used by the worklist

### Blocks A and B: the reduced problem

- **Critical point:** a unit $`u`$ with $`\nabla h(u) = 4h(u)\,u`$, the gradient taken in the real coordinates of $`V_j`$.
- **$`K_u`$:** the tangent space at $`u`$ of the orbit $`\Gamma u`$; **$`k = \dim K_u`$**, over the reals.
- **$`\widehat M_u = \mathrm{Hess}\,h(u) - 4h(u)`$**, a symmetric real-linear map of $`E_0`$, which vanishes on $`K_u`$.
- **$`N_u`$:** the orthogonal complement of $`u`$ and $`K_u`$. The **signature** $`(n_-, n_0, n_+)`$ of the point is that of $`\widehat M_u`$ on $`N_u`$.
- **$`r_u`$:** the dimension of the radical of $`\sigma`$ restricted to $`K_u`$.
- **$`\widehat S_u = i\widehat M_u`$**, the slow matrix, a real-linear map of $`E_0`$. Its **zero multiplicity** is the algebraic multiplicity of 0 as a root of its characteristic polynomial. Its **slow polynomial** is that characteristic polynomial with the zero roots removed, written in $`\nu = s^2`$.
- **Clusters and Krein signs:** a nonzero eigenvalue pair $`\pm i\tau`$ of $`\widehat S_u`$ has the real invariant subspace $`\ker(\widehat S_u^2 + \tau^2)`$. Its Krein sign is $`+`$ or $`-`$ when $`\widehat M_u`$ is positive or negative definite there, and the cluster is indefinite otherwise.
- **The slow-flow words**, read from the slow polynomial's roots:
  - *elliptic*: every root is a negative real; then *simple*, if every root is simple, or *one double frequency*, if exactly one root is double and the rest are simple;
  - *hyperbolic* otherwise: *one real pair*, if there is no non-real root and exactly one positive root, simple; *real pairs*, if there is no non-real root and the positive roots have total multiplicity at least 2; *a complex quartet*, if there is no positive root and exactly one conjugate pair of non-real roots, each simple.
- **In $`R_2`$, at a member with complex structure $`\tau`$:** $`\widehat S_u^\tau = \tau\widehat M_u`$ in place of $`i\widehat M_u`$, with its clusters and Krein signs read the same way.

### Block C: beyond leading order

- **$`\lambda_0 = n(n+2)`$**, the eigenvalue at the slot's lowest level $`n`$, and $`\lambda_L = L(L+2)`$ at level $`L`$; $`L_0 = -\Delta - \lambda_0`$, inverted on the complement of $`E_0`$, where $`P_\perp`$ projects.
- **$`N(\psi) = \lvert\psi\rvert^2\psi`$**, pointwise, and for $`y \in V_j`$
```math
\widetilde R_0(y) \;=\; -\big\langle L_0^{-1} P_\perp N(\psi_y),\; P_\perp N(\psi_y)\big\rangle \;=\; -\sum_{L} \frac{\lVert \Pi_L N(\psi_y)\rVert^2}{\lambda_L - \lambda_0} ,
```
  the sum over the levels $`L`$ above the lowest at which the slot occurs, with $`\Pi_L`$ the orthogonal projection onto level $`L`$ and the inner product of $`L^2(X)`$. The **level norms** are the $`\lVert\Pi_L N(\psi_y)\rVert^2`$.
- **$`\kappa_u = h(u)`$** at a unit critical $`u`$, with $`V = 1`$ (generally $`\kappa_u = Q(u)/V`$).
- **At a degenerate point:** $`K' = \ker\widehat M_u \cap N_u`$, and
```math
\gamma_u \;=\; \kappa_u^{-2}\big(\mathrm{Hess}\,\widetilde R_0(u) - 6\,\widetilde R_0(u)\big)\big|_{K'} ,
```
  relative to the inner product, reported as a multiple of $`R^2`$ when it is a multiple of the identity.
- **$`\Gamma_u`$:** the subgroup of $`\Gamma`$ that fixes $`u`$, counted as maps of $`E_0`$: two elements that act on $`E_0`$ by the same map count once. At $`v_1`$ the **circle** is $`\theta \mapsto R_z(\theta)\,e^{i\theta}`$, the rotation about $`z`$ by $`\theta`$ combined with the phase $`e^{i\theta}`$, where $`R_z(\theta)`$ acts on $`E_0`$ by $`w_m \mapsto e^{-im\theta}w_m`$ on the Condon–Shortley components.
- **At a nondegenerate point:** $`\hat z \in \mathbb{R}u \oplus N_u`$ solves $`\widehat M_u\hat z = \nabla\widetilde R_0(u)`$ there, and
```math
D_u \;=\; \kappa_u^{-2}\big(\mathrm{Hess}\,\widetilde R_0(u) - D^3h(u)[\hat z]\big)
```
  in the normalization $`V = R = 1`$. With $`\omega_0 = c\sqrt{\lambda_0}`$ and $`\chi = c^2/(8\omega_0 Q(u))`$, a **slow group** $`g`$ is a nonzero eigenvalue $`s_g`$ of $`\widehat S_u`$, with $`\mu_0 = \chi s_g`$.
- **A formula to evaluate**, not a definition of $`c_g`$:
```math
T_1 \;=\; \frac{c^2}{4\omega_0}\, iD_u \;-\; \frac{c^2}{2\omega_0^2}\,\mu_0 \;+\; \frac{\mu_0^2}{2\omega_0}\, i ,
```
  and, with right and left eigenvectors $`r`$ and $`\ell`$ of $`\widehat S_u`$ for $`s_g`$ normalized by $`\ell^{\mathsf T} r = 1`$, $`c_g = \ell^{\mathsf T} T_1 r`$. For a semisimple $`s_g`$ of multiplicity $`m`$, with $`\ell_a^{\mathsf T} r_b = \delta_{ab}`$, the values are the eigenvalues of $`C_{ab} = \ell_a^{\mathsf T} T_1 r_b`$. A value of this formula is all that is asked; whether it is a coefficient of anything is not.

### Block D: the certificates

The function is $`h`$ of Section 1.3 in $`R_4`$ or $`R_5`$, in the chart of the 14 real coordinates $`x = (\mathrm{Re}\,p_3, \ldots, \mathrm{Re}\,p_{-3}, \mathrm{Im}\,p_3, \ldots, \mathrm{Im}\,p_{-3})`$, with the diagonal metric $`G`$ for which $`\lvert x\rvert_G = \lVert w\rVert`$; $`\nabla h`$ below is the vector of partial derivatives $`\partial h/\partial x_a`$, not $`G^{-1}`$ of it. Let $`X_1, \ldots, X_4`$ be real-linear generators of the phase and the three infinitesimal rotations in this chart: `inputs.json`, "generators", gives the realization the bench used, as exact $`14 \times 14`$ matrices, and any basis of the same four-dimensional span gives the same slice and the same solution, with $`c = 0`$. Let $`x_0`$ a box's start point (Section 5), with $`k_i = X_i x_0`$, constant vectors. The **slice system** is 19 equations in the unknowns $`(x, \lambda, c_1, \ldots, c_4)`$:

```math
\nabla h(x) - \lambda\, G x - \sum_i c_i\, G k_i \;=\; 0 , \qquad \lvert x\rvert_G^2 = 1 , \qquad \langle G k_i,\, x - x_0\rangle = 0 \quad (i = 1, \ldots, 4) .
```

A **box** is the product of the intervals $`[\,t - 10^{-60}, t + 10^{-60}\,]`$ over all 19 unknowns, about the centre $`t`$ whose $`x`$-part Section 5 gives, whose $`c`$-part is $`0`$, and whose $`\lambda`$-part you compute from your own $`h`$ as $`\lambda = 4h(x)/\lvert x\rvert_G^2`$ at the given $`x`$.

### Block E: the rigid families

For a slot $`\rho`$, the trivial one included, whose lowest level $`n`$ gives $`V_{n/2}`$ restricted to $`\Gamma_0`$ equal to $`\rho`$, every lowest-level section is the row $`\psi_v(g) = v^{\mathsf T}\pi(g)`$ up to the identification, with $`\pi`$ the representation on $`V_{n/2}`$ and $`\lvert\psi_v\rvert = \lvert v\rvert`$ constant. Then $`e^{i\omega T}\psi_v`$ solves the field equation for every $`v`$, with $`\omega^2/c^2 = n(n+2)/R^2 + g_4\lvert v\rvert^2`$: a **member** of the rigid family. Put $`\psi = e^{i\omega T}(\psi_v + \zeta)`$, linearize the field equation in $`\zeta`$ as a first-order system in $`(\zeta, \partial_T\zeta)`$, and split $`\zeta`$ into its part in $`E_0`$ and the rest.
