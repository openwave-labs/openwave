**What a classical relaxation can and cannot say.** A relaxation reads a static field, its energy, and where a texture goes under one descent. It cannot say what sign a quantum state takes under an exchange: that depends on the loops of the space of configurations and on a quantization rule, which no descent reads. A descent is not a time evolution; the route it takes depends on the optimizer, so the route words of rung 1 that enter a statement are read with two optimizers (§ 5); the chains of rungs 2 and 3, and their TORN words, are read with one. ### 1. The turn, the zero mode, the swap and the start rule

The action is that of M5.51 § 1, unchanged (`m5_51_action.py`): $\kappa = v = L_V = 1$, $L_1 = 0$, $\delta = 0$, the midpoint link weight, faces held.

**The symmetry.** For a constant rotation $Q$: $[Q A Q^{\mathsf T}, Q B Q^{\mathsf T}] = Q [A, B] Q^{\mathsf T}$, norms and $\operatorname{tr} M^k$ are kept, a difference of turned matrices is the turned difference, and $s\big(\tfrac12 (Q M_i Q^{\mathsf T} + Q M_j Q^{\mathsf T})\big) = s\big(\tfrac12 (M_i + M_j)\big)$. So every term of $U$ that couples only nodes turned by one $Q$ is unchanged, on the lattice exactly (C1). A turn that varies from node to node keeps every node's eigenvalues, so $U_V$ is unchanged; it changes $U_4$ and $U_{LV}$.

**The collar turn.** For an angle $\Theta$, a unit axis $a$, a center $c$ and radii $R_1 \lt R_2$:

$$
T[\Theta, a, c, R_1, R_2] : \quad M(n) \mapsto Q(n) M(n) Q(n)^{\mathsf T} , \qquad Q(n) = \exp\big( \Theta f(\rho_n) [a]_{\times} \big) , \qquad \rho_n = \lvert x_n - c \rvert , \qquad f(\rho) = \min\Big( 1, \max\Big( 0, \frac{R_2 - \rho}{R_2 - R_1} \Big) \Big)
$$

For $\Theta$ a multiple of 2π, $Q = \mathbb{1}$ wherever $\rho \le R_1$ or $\rho \ge R_2$: the core, the faces and every node outside the collar are those of the state, exactly (C2). The profile is linear (a cosine was weighed and set aside): for $n$ near the radial unit vector, the turned field pairs a radial derivative of size $\theta'$ with tangential ones of size $1/\rho$, so the collar's quartic is $C \int \theta'^2 d\rho$, least for a linear $\theta$, at $C \Theta^2 / (R_2 - R_1)$ (an argument of this plan, in the continuum); the linear profile also has the smaller largest turn per link.

**⭐ The zero mode of a free core.** Read $T[\psi, a, c, R_1, R_2]$ for $\psi$ from $2\pi$ down to 0. Every node with $\rho \le R_1$, the ring included, takes the one turn of the target $R_a(\psi)$, positions kept, which costs nothing inside the ball (no term of the action pairs a matrix index with a spatial one, so a uniform turn of the target alone is a symmetry; the elastic term $L_1 \lVert \partial M \rVert^2$ of the earlier actions is unchanged by it too, so the zero mode does not rest on $L_1 = 0$); every node with $\rho \ge R_2$ is untouched; only the collar's terms change, and at $\psi = 0$ the field is the reference. So with the core free, every turned start is joined to its reference by a continuous path along which every node keeps its eigenvalues. At plan time, on fixed fields, on `G6_mid_a` ($R_1 = 4.354$, $R_2 = 11$), ΔU along the path at $\psi/\pi = 2, 1.875, \dots, 0.125, 0$:

**The swap.** About the box center, with the same $f$: $S[R_1, R_2] : M(x) \mapsto M\big( R_z(\pi f(\rho))^{\mathsf T} x \big)$, by trilinear interpolation of the six components. Inside $R_1$ it is the node map $(x, y, z) \mapsto (-x, -y, z)$, which exchanges the rings; outside $R_2$ the identity; both exact (C7). For a field equivariant about $z$ with weight 2, $M(R_z(\alpha) x) = R_z(2\alpha) M(x) R_z(2\alpha)^{\mathsf T}$, so

$$
M\big( R_z(\alpha)^{\mathsf T} x \big) = R_z(-2\alpha) M(x) R_z(-2\alpha)^{\mathsf T}
$$

and $S$ equals $T[-2\pi, \hat z, 0, R_1, R_2]$ there: the half-turn that exchanges the pair leaves a full internal turn across the collar. ### 5. The words, frozen

$U_{\mathrm{ref}}$ is the energy of the reference (H1: `G6_mid_a`; H2, H3: the end of B2 or B3; P: the pair end), ΔU $= U_{\mathrm{end}} - U_{\mathrm{ref}}$. "The readers agree": U within $10^{-5}$ relative of $U_{\mathrm{ref}}$, every $R_L$ within 0.01 ($\tau_L$ of M5.51) and every center within 0.05 (the plan's choice), the same class and degree, one ring in regime (TWO RINGS on P); for row 21 against the pair end or its half-turn image, whichever is nearer.
