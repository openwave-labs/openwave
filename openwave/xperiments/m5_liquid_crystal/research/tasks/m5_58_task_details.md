## TASK PLANNING (2026-11-25; opened, not staged: the plan is written and audited before any go)

**Scope.** The post lists three runs asked of OpenWave, each with a prediction the author states as registered before anyone runs it. All three are dynamic runs on a kicked or rotating core, which is the ground of the lepton series ([M5.21.6](m5_21_6_task_details.md), [M5.21.10](m5_21_10_task_details.md), [M5.21.9](m5_21_9_task_details.md)), of the tracer of [M5.23.2](m5_23_2_task_details.md) and of the kicked neutron analog of [M5.22.2](m5_22_2_task_details.md).

**What the capstone document adds (version 4.6 as deposited under the DOI of the post, items 9 and 28; evidence, not resolution).** Item 9 describes the lepton runs above, what they found and what failed, as read at a commit of 2026-11-24; the energy ratios it quotes for the three states are those of [M5.21.2b](m5_21_2b_task_details.md). It then places the decay film in the action of version 4.6 (the gradient term on time derivatives only, and a spectral potential) with the three states as three cores in one exterior, and says that the mechanism of the OpenWave runs carries over while their numbers do not. Neither that action nor a heavy core in the exterior of the lowest state is on OpenWave: the film of M5.21.6 and M5.21.10 ran on the action of that series, with each state in its own exterior. The document assigns its own round on these runs to the author's side, with two predictions (a core of the middle state's type in the exterior of the lowest state is a local minimum distinct from the lowest; a kicked heavy core decays to the lowest state and emits two compact closed loops of different classes). The label RL-1 of the post is not in the document; the second of those predictions is the nearest to it.

**The author's answer (2026-11-26, [discussioncomment-19911695](https://github.com/vantasnerdan/substrate-framework/discussions/186#discussioncomment-19911695)).** The three reads run first on the action and the states where the decay film already exists, which are those of the lepton series above. In the author's words the coframe action of versions 4.6 and 4.7 "isn't ready for a port yet: no stationary core and no full isospin"; they cite item 29 of capstone 4.7, a version released later that day (that item states that with the spectral potential held constant no stationary core of unit baryon number exists, and that on the real orbit it uses isospin is only an axial U(1)), and will send equations, parameters and a reference state when it is ready. One reader is asked, which the author says would decide FD-1: the axis class of each ejected filament, "which eigenvector its director winds about", at the moment it leaves the core. On the energy ratios 1 : 4.2 : 16.0 the author confirms that they are the census of [M5.21.2b](m5_21_2b_task_details.md) on the stencil-symmetrized functional, and says the attribution in the document is to be corrected.

**Open at the plan.** The action and the states are the author's answer above. To be fixed by the plan, each with a known-answer control: the heavy state and the kick, those of [M5.21.6](m5_21_6_task_details.md) and [M5.21.10](m5_21_10_task_details.md) or others; what counts as a closed loop pinched off by reconnection, as a rule of the tracer; the axis class of an ejected filament as a reader, and the instant a filament leaves the core; which mode is the slow precession, and how a 1:3 lock is told from a transient. A port of the coframe action is a separate, later task, opened when its equations, parameters and reference state arrive, as [M5.51](m5_51_task_details.md) ported the ring.
