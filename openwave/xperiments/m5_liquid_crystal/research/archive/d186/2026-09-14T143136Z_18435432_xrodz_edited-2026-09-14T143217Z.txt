Not this rung: the sigma term (v12, the R22 candidate with its own certification), v23 and v24 (new field content), `inc(h) = 2 G(h)` and the sourced-constraint Newton (nothing to run here), the `J != 0` reading (accepted as the reading of our own saddle).

## Objects and boundaries

The three axes of R20 (`S_1 (1, delta, 0)`, `S_d (delta, 1, 0)`, `S_0 (0, 1, delta)` on `4 I1 + V4`, g 8, delta 0.3, the same seeds and instrument) under:

```text
B_seed   the R20 boundary: the Dirichlet shell (depth 1.6) at the seed's values, the seed's line defect frozen at half-width 24
B_far    the Dirichlet shell at the relaxed far field: the R20 n64 end field's values on the n32 box's shell (the central 32^3 of the 64^3 field)
```

Pre-registered: the seed's line defect is intrinsic to the three-axis assignment (the frame `(n, phi, theta)` is singular on the polar axis for every assignment, the cost is the transverse eigenvalue carried by `phi` or `theta`), so no pinned boundary is fair to the three; the free boundary is the one that does not hold the defect, and with `pi_2 = 0` on the biaxial vacuum it can let the string escape through the faces or the electron fill the box, both of which are outcomes with names below.
