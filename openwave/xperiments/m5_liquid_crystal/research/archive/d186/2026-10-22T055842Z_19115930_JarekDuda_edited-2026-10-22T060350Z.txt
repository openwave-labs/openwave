**Cosmological criteria for the chart, anchored on a fitted cyclic background**

**The target.** [Little red dots: detailed analysis](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22666156) v11.0, Appendices L–N, fits the reversible background

- r(a) = Ω_r a⁻⁴ + Ω_m a⁻³ + Ω_Λ − Q a², with E² = r(1 − r/B) and a loop-quantum-cosmology (LQC) bounce at ρ_c = 0.41 ρ_Pl,
- against DESI DR2 BAO, Pantheon+ supernovae and compressed CMB distances.

Results:

- Q = 0.067, Δχ² = −2.54 for one extra parameter (ΔAIC −0.54). That is compatible, but no preference; Q = 0 remains allowed.
- Turnaround at a = 3.38; bounces 87 Gyr apart.
- Today w₀ = −0.935 and w_a ≈ −0.14. This is DESI's direction, but smaller.
- The dark component is a canonical scalar with φ̇² = (2/3)ρ₀Q a². Since recombination it has rolled Δφ = 0.19 M̄_Pl, and 0.15 M̄_Pl since z = 1.

An LdGS cosmology must supply that scalar as a mode of M, the bounce in place of LQC, cold dark matter and the radiation content. One structural fact applies throughout: for a homogeneous field, F_μν = 0, so −F² − V gives the vacuum no cosmological dynamics. A quadratic kinetic term (e.g. κ tr(∂M ∂M), as in R27) is a prerequisite for every row below.
