## TL;DR

1. **The term.** Inside `V(tr N^p)` exactly one linear invariant `L` keeps `(g, 1, delta, delta)` critical, and the split `delta ± eps` costs exactly `(g + delta)(1 - delta) c eps^2 - c eps^4 / 2` under `V4 - c L`. Five zero modes plus the doublet at `5.81 c`. One finding the plan did not foresee: on full 4x4 matrices a second signature sector lies lower than the vacuum, behind a barrier.
2. **The halo.** The stiffness does what the 09-19 reply predicted qualitatively: it confines the biaxial halo and restores a clean unit hedgehog for `c >= 1e-3`. With `c = 0` a fresh seed relaxes to the R22 box-filling state again.
3. **The exponent.** Not certified, by either law. The pre-registered label is `HALO_BOX_LIMITED`. Over the seven-point ladder requested on 09-20 the readable window holds three points and only one of them is clean: there is a wall at `c <= 1e-4` on both boxes, an amplitude threshold between `c` 3e-3 and 1e-2, and a collapse of a disclination network between `c` 3e-4 and 1e-3. On the linear tail alone the profile follows the `c^(-1/4)` equation to 9 to 18 percent: a hypothesis on three points, not a result.
4. **Delta 0.9224.** Both rows fill the box: at `W1 x 25` the hedgehog tears, at the scaled weight it melts into a scale-free ramp. My stated expectation (row A reads the lattice) was wrong. Row B cannot take a stiffness at all: the vacuum is unstable there above `c` 1e-6.
5. **For Maciej.** The well of the 008 ladder survives the positive contraction because the report's contraction already is the positive one; it disappears under `eta`. More important: any non-uniform field has such a well, so its existence carries no information.

On that field `M_0i = 0` exactly and `G` equals the Euclidean metric to 3.9e-4, so the report's contraction already is the positive one: label `WELL_SURVIVES`. The static density lives entirely in the rotation block and the frozen tangent's density entirely in the boost block (`a0` has only `0i` entries), so under `eta` the second flips sign and the well is gone. That is the 09-19 hypothesis reversed: the well is there because the contraction is positive on the boost block. "No well under `eta`" holds on an exactly block-diagonal field only (white-noise `M_0i` of amplitude 1e-2 moves `C1(eta)` to +4.1e-5).

The point that matters for the ladder: under a positive contraction `C1` is a sum of products of non-negative densities, so ANY non-uniform field has a well. Three block-diagonal white-noise fields with no soliton return `omega_E` of 25.3, 25.6, 24.9; the uniform vacuum returns `C1 = 0`. Existence is fixed by the form `(i1s - omega^2 k1)^2`; only the magnitude could inform, and R22-3 showed the magnitude depends on the `a0` normalization, the envelope and `h^(-3/2)`. The released-profile test and `(Q, E, dE/dQ)` need your time-dependent stack and were not run here.
