<p><strong>2. κ_s is now pinned down to a fork</strong> (Sol, replayed independently).</p>
<ul>
<li>An induced hadronic value is 0.009, far too small.</li>
<li>Standard-Model-size matter over a Planck window gives 2.0, and the measured weak coupling 1/g₂² gives 2.35.</li>
<li>So the proton is protected only if the spectator curvature has weak, not hadronic, strength. The selection rule is then B − L together with B mod 3 (ΔB = ΔL = 3), as in the Standard Model.</li>
<li>Hence the fork: give the coframe's left index independent gauge links with the measured couplings, or induce them, which needs charged matter beyond the Standard Model.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>3. The road</strong> (each step's pass criterion is written down before it runs):</p>
<ul>

<li>The test for (i): a neutral B = 1 core must carry time-line Chern density, zero in total, that gives the neutron's r_n² = −0.1155 fm² and μ_n = −1.913 μ_N. Our current core has none, so (i) fails as it stands.</li>
</ul>
</li>
