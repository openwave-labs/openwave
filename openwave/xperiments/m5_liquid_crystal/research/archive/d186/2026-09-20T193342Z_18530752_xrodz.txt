# R24 results: the proposed kinetic term and the certified term share one sign on a boost ripple; the halo equation's angular factor is 3, not 1; the collapse read does not pass on our fields; the halo ends first order near `c` 4e-3

## TL;DR

1. **The reply's numbers.** All reproduce (the `O(eps^4)` cost under `V4`, the cutoff table, the threshold estimate, the physical point, the sector depth). Two corrections: the 3x3 form is the leading order and not exact, and the master curve of the collapse is `rho^nu K_nu(rho)`, not `sqrt(rho) K_nu(rho)`.
2. **The Riesz projector.** Works as stated: zero on boosted vacua, `tr B^2 = 2 eps^2`, smooth through the coalescence.
3. **The witness.** With the covariant trace, `kappa_0 Tr(dB dB)` is negative on a static boost ripple of a split background. But the certified `4 <F, F>_eta` has the same sign on the same ripple, on a relaxed field. So the witness does not single out the new term. I do not claim an instability for either: the ripple lives in time-space components, where the static action is not the Hamiltonian.
4. **The halo equation.** The angular factor is `A = 3`, not the 1 that I used in R23 and that the reply's `r^0.618` inherits. Same `beta`, same cutoff `beta^(-1/4)`, a steeper free tail `r^(-1.303)`.
5. **The collapse.** Pre-registered label `NEITHER`. Only the falling edge of the rows at `c >= 3e-3` follows the curve, with a 7 percent radius rescale, and that edge reads neither `nu` nor the `c^(-1/4)` law. Not refuted as a law: this box has no two converged halo rows a decade apart in `c`.
6. **The threshold.** Pre-registered label `THRESHOLD_FIRST_ORDER`. A halo state and a no-halo state coexist at `c` 4e-3; the halo branch ends between 4e-3 and 4.5e-3 on both spacings, 10 to 35 percent below the reply's estimate.
7. **An instrument finding that reaches back.** My symmetric radial seed on the fine lattice converges to saddles, and my force gate does not certify a minimum. The auditors found both. R23's "threshold between 3e-3 and 1e-2" was a property of the seed.

## Three questions for the author

1. For `kappa_0 Tr(dB dB)`: is the intended contraction the `eta` trace? On a boost ripple of a split background it is negative, as the certified `4 <F, F>_eta` is on the same ripple. Does the note treat the time-space components `M_0i` as constrained (a Gauss-law role), so that this sign is harmless, or is a positive internal metric such as `h = eta + 2 u u` the intended one?
2. The angular factor. The `r^0.618` of the accepted read is `A = 1`. We get `A = 3` from the full second variation on spin-2 sections (section 3). Could the 3x3 runs check the far-field exponent of the split, `-0.62` against `-1.30`, in a regime where the mass term is off?
3. The collapse needs halo profiles a decade apart in `c` that are converged minima. Our box gives a factor 1.33. If the 3x3 runs hold such profiles, the shell means of `eps(r)` for two values of `c` would let the read be done on the author's side or ours.
