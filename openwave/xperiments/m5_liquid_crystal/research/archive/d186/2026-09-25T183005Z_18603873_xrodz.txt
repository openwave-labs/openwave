## 0. The stack and the norm

Real symmetric 4x4 `M(x)`, `eta = diag(-1, 1, 1, 1)`, `N = M eta`, the certified biaxial vacuum `diag(8, 1, delta, 0)`. The static energy

    E = 4 h^3 sum_{i<j} <F_ij, F_ij>_eta + V4,   F_ij = [A_i, A_j]_eta = A_i eta A_j - A_j eta A_i,   A_i = d_i M,
    <F, G>_eta = tr(eta F eta G^T),   V4 = w sum_{p<=4} (tr N^p - C_p)^2,   w = W1 x 25 unless said.

The tangent of `M -> Lambda M Lambda^T` is `dM = G M + M G^T` (symmetric, `G eta + eta G^T = 0`): the commutator for a rotation, the anticommutator for a boost; the lower-index form of the reply is the same formula with `G' = G^T` (sympy, all six generators). The block-diagonal static sector `M_0i = 0` is where every static number lives; there the eta and Frobenius contractions coincide to round-off, and on the full-kick witness fields they differ by the sign (item 1 of the TL;DR).

## 1. R26-2: the strand's delta ladder, and what sits below the bound

**The deviation form.** `M = M0 + delta D`; the curvature is homogeneous of degree 4 in first derivatives, so `E_u[M] = delta^4 E_u[D]`; the trace brackets are telescoped, `tr N^p - tr N0^p = sum_k tr(N^k eps N0^{p-1-k})`, `eps = N - N0`, so the `8^p` of the time slot is never formed and subtracted (the plain form's relative round-off 4e-7 at delta 0.001, the telescoped 8e-9; the audit's mpmath reference 2e-6 against 1e-15). `T_slaved3` is the three-slot Bogomolny integral `4 pi sqrt(8) int_0^{f0} sqrt(w V_min(f)) df` with the same binomial brackets; it agrees with the R25-1 audit's plain-form integral to 1e-11 where that one still has digits.

**The label and the audit.** My pre-registered rule returned `STRAND_0258_UNRESOLVED` (the delta 0.03 and 0.01 rows at h 1 and below did not reach the gate in 4000 iterations). The audit refuted the instrument's gate: the delta 0.003 and 0.001 rows were passed by a gate set at ten times the round-off floor while 0.7 to 1.5 percent of energy remained (the block problem is scale invariant to `O(delta)`, so they must sit where the delta 0.01 rows sit, and the audit's rescaled twins and its own descents land there), and the FALLING rows are converged to 1e-5, their deficit the BPS seed's lattice factor (0.9858, 0.9936, 0.9964, 0.9984 at the four spacings). The rule inverted the evidence; the number is the audit's: `1.000 +- 0.001` at every delta. Two instrument corrections for the next ladder: the gate read against the pair-slot gradient in scaled units, and a deviation-native optimizer state at delta 0.003 and below.

**What sits below the bound.** The scaled variables need a diagonal preconditioner for L-BFGS (the diagonal slots' stiffness is delta-independent while the strand's is `O(1)`; the condition number 1e11 at delta 0.001). That optimizer, seeded on R25-1's stored block strands with no kick beyond their own director-row residue (1e-4 to 1e-3 of delta), leaves the block sector: the director row reaches 0.5 delta and the energy falls to 0.34 (delta 0.3) and 0.52 (delta 0.03) of `T_bps` in 1500 iterations, 0.13 to 0.16 at 8000, still falling; constrained to the block sector it still falls to 0.57. Interpolated to a grid twice as fine these fields rise to 2 to 23 `T_bps` (the block strands keep their value), which I first read as a lattice artifact; the audit re-relaxed the refined field at h / 2 and it came back to 0.26 `T_bps` in 400 iterations. The texture: pi-jump walls of the pair anisotropy (7 to 107 bonds with angle jumps over 150 degrees) with the core UNMELTED (`b / b0` 0.77 to 0.98 on the axis). Across a wall `M` jumps, but `[A_x, A_y]_eta` vanishes where the field is constant along the wall and `V4` is blind to a rotation; the pure winding with an unmelted point core has `u = 32 (b b' / rho)^2 = 0` in the continuum as well. The Bogomolny bound needs a continuous core, which nothing in the functional enforces. Hessian probes on the audit's own gradient: `lambda_min` -1.0e-5 in the director-row subspace and -2.6e-3 inside the block sector (a cell-scale mode) at delta 0.03, h 1; the director-row mode carried to h 0.5 keeps a negative Rayleigh quotient (the delta 0.3 probes did not converge). The pure winding with no melted core and NO walls is not the state (it costs 11.6 to 115 `T_bps` at h 1.5 to 0.5, as `1 / h^2`).

So: 0.258 is the stack's number for the smooth-core strand; under the certified action that class is a saddle, and wall textures sit below it at every spacing tried. Whether a smoothness term (a Dirichlet piece, or the Skyrme-like terms of Q13) is the intended repair is yours to say.
