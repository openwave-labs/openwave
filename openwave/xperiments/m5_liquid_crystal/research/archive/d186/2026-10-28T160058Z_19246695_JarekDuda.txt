1. Δ and the tail normalization.

For the uniaxial director estimate, define
[
M_{\rm sp}=\lambda_\perp I+\Delta_{\rm dir}nn^T,
\qquad
\Delta_{\rm dir}=\lambda_{\rm long}-\lambda_\perp.
]
Thus (1,\delta,\delta) gives \Delta_{\rm dir}=1-\delta. This is neither an E singular-value gap nor the minor-axis biaxial splitting.

With the certified L=-4I_1-V, my radial check gives
[
I_1=\frac{2\Delta_{\rm dir}^4}{r^4},
\qquad
C_{\rm tail}\equiv R E(r>R)=32\pi\Delta_{\rm dir}^4.
]
If retaining c=16\pi\Delta_{\rm dir}^4, explicitly defining c=C_{\rm tail}/2 would settle the factor of two. I am not asserting that the previously undefined c already meant this.

At fixed action coefficient and units,
[
\delta\ln C_{\rm tail}
=4\frac{\delta\lambda_{\rm long}-\delta\lambda_\perp}{\Delta_{\rm dir}}.

This applies on the uniaxial branch. The certified spatial vacuum (1,\delta,0) is biaxial; no single-gap substitution has been established there. Please retain the registered pairwise-gap fallback, without labelling it an α response before fixing the electric normalization.

2. The rank-three soldering convention is acceptable for the aligned diagnostic.

Please retain the separate null-leg a₁ norm and convention-dependence flag. The pseudo-inverse is an additional definition, not a unique limit of invertible tetrads.

which agrees with the diagonal pseudo-inverse at E₀. This is a covariance check, not a request to change the aligned scan.

Our (1/4,1/2,-1) prediction remains registered and falsifiable. Its usual TEGR interpretation does not by itself establish GR for internal δ, background η and rank-three E.
