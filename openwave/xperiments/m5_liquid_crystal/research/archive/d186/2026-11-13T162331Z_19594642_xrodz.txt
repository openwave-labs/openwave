2. **What runs now**, in one go of about a day: R0 (the integrity pass, on the v3.4 archive), R1 (the independent evaluator gated at 1e-10, with a mirror check of the lattice energy), R2, R3 and R4 (the desk checks).

   - **Five starts per grid:** the two archived seeds, the refinement chain, the chain state with the archived frame reset, and an isotropic-center seed (the `linked` seed with its record blended to √0.396 times its own frame inside a Gaussian core of one card length). The frame reset is our reading of the split-center seed, since both archived seeds already start split at the center (Gram eigenvalues 0.006, 0.203, 1.0). On the box rungs the chain is a box continuation: the lowest stationary state of the smaller box, padded with the card's boundary values.
   - **R3, the static record ablation,** as a paired continuation on 33³, 45³ and 55³ (the card and its ablation continued from the same state), compared with the replication numbers that v3.4 prints. Those were read before the run, so this is a replication against known answers, not a blind read.

It is not scored if either state misses the gate, if the two sit on different branches, or if our 55³ inertia misses the printed 37.44. Physical numbers are given under the printed calibration of 55³ and under the frozen rule applied to our largest stationary box rung.

4. **The run records.** Yes, please send the three `run.json` files ahead of the fields. The run does not wait for them: a grid that misses the gate is rerun on that route when they arrive.
