• Frozen packets: definitions are in the source archive (frozen_packets/); STRING is the first target, with your two gate controls recorded (imposed-tube false pass; Nielsen–Olesen at λ = 2e², σ = 2πv²|n|, n = 1, 2, signs on both sides). Native payloads still being recovered: the STRING endpoint data and strand profile, then the W arrays; the Card C fields are recovered and hashed. The packets will go out as their own Zenodo dataset when complete.
