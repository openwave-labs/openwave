# M5.41 on Card C: twelve routes end under the gate, seven meet the rule as frozen, and the zero-spacing values are a trend

## TL;DR

1. Thank you for confirming the readings, and for v3.96. The run on OpenWave is done as frozen: twelve routes on four meshes at half-box 6.075.
2. All twelve routes end on a saved last stop under the gate, with no restart. Seven meet the rule as frozen; two hold lumps and three miss the 10⁻⁴ bound by 10 to 92 percent.
3. 65³ has one route that meets the rule. By item 8 of the plan the word calibration is withheld, and the numbers are a trend: e 4.222, an effective f_π of 93.2 MeV, g_A 0.913 at zero spacing and half-box 6.075.
4. Between routes the half range of the inertia and of g_A e² is under 10⁻⁴ on every mesh. The form of the fit moves the inertia thirteen times more than the choice of route.
5. One question for the next run (section 3): the clause on the two stops a factor 10 apart can be read in two other ways, and under one of them nearly the same numbers would be a calibration. This run stays a trend under the reading you confirmed.
6. Two things the rule does not see (section 6): the lump check is blind at the core, and no route is at the lowest energy on record for 65³ and 77³.

## 1. What was computed

With (p, f) the stored unknowns, q = p / |p|, E the card's energy and h the spacing, on the interior nodes:

```text
gate        G = max( max |dE/dp| |p| , max |dE/df| ) / h^3       read at every iterate; a stop is under the gate when G <= 1e-3
stops       targets 1000, 3000, 10000, 30000 in the route's own iterations; the state saved for a target is the
            first iterate at or after it with G <= 1e-3; if none comes before the next target, the iterate at the
            target count is kept; the route ends at the first such iterate at or after 30000 (or at 33000)
pair        the last stop, and the latest earlier saved stop whose iteration is at most a tenth of the last stop's
fixed       for X in (inertia, g_A e^2, mu_V): |X_stop - X_last| / |X_last| <= 1e-4 for the pair stop and for the 10000 stop
lump        a node with  lambda_max(f^T f) - (1 - q_0)/2 > 0.2
meets       last stop under the gate, no lump on it, the three readouts fixed
spread      (max - min)/2 over the routes' last stops on a mesh, over their mean
line        X(h) = X_0 + b h^2 by least squares through 55^3, 65^3, 77^3 on the means of the routes that meet the rule
route range (max - min)/2 of X_0 over every combination of one such route per mesh
frozen rule e_0^2 = 2 I_0 Delta L / (3 hbar c),  f_pi = hbar c / (L e_0),  unit = f_pi / e_0,  g_A = (g_A e^2)_0 / e_0^2
            mu_V at the gap Delta: the script's mu_V times the state's own gap over Delta, then the line
            own gap = (3/2) (4.259^3 x 92.4 MeV) / I,   Delta = 293 MeV,   hbar c / L = 4.259 x 92.4 MeV
```

The readouts are those of M5.40, unchanged.

## 3. The labels, and one question

Six routes take as their pair stop a state that was saved by count and was not under the gate (the daggered stops at 1000 or 3000, gates of 7 × 10⁻³ to 9 × 10⁻²). Item 2 of the plan saves the first iterate under the gate; on five of these routes that pushed the 3000 stop past a tenth of the last one, and on 77³ `smooth` the 3000 stop itself was saved by count. Two of the seven routes that meet the rule, both on 77³, pass against such a state. The same stops read three ways:

| (i) as frozen: the latest earlier saved stop at most a tenth of the last stop's iteration, under the gate or not | `chain`, `smooth`, your field | `chain` | `iso`, `chain` | 7 | no |
| (ii) the same, among the stops that were under the gate | `chain`, `smooth`, your field | `chain` | none | 5 | no |
| (iii) the stop of the 3000 target, at whatever iteration it was saved | `iso`, `chain`, `smooth`, your field | `iso`, `chain` | `iso`, `chain` | 9 | yes |

Every label of this post and of the record uses (i), the reading of the plan that you confirmed, and this run stays a trend under it. Reading (iii) counts the factor 10 in targets, not in iterations: its pairs are 3.3 to 10 times apart, 3.6 on 65³ `iso`, the route that would give 65³ its second. Under (iii) the line over 16 combinations gives an inertia of 35.9144, e 4.22214, an effective f_π of 93.207 MeV and g_A 0.91338, inside the route range of section 5.

**The question.** Which reading should the rule carry in the next run: (i), (ii) or (iii)? Under (ii) 77³ has no route with a valid pair, and under (iii) the pair is 3.6 to 6.8 times apart on four of the nine routes that would meet the rule. Two of the seven routes that meet the rule are within 20 percent of the bound (77³ `iso`, μ_V at 8.9 × 10⁻⁵; 55³ `smooth`, the inertia at 8.4 × 10⁻⁵), and three pair stops sit at exactly a tenth of the last one.

## 6. What the rule does not see

1. **Lumps at the core.** The lump check measures the largest record eigenvalue against (1 − q₀)/2, which is near 1 at the core, so a node left near one is not flagged there. A read against a node's six neighbors ([`m5_41_spikes.py`](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/eaf6d8b3239b4f38f6ba6212fb80728d5672457a/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/scripts/m5_41_spikes.py#L23)) finds two such nodes next to the center on 77³ `smooth` (eigenvalue 0.956, radius 0.23), beside its two flagged ones, and none on the 40 stops of the ten routes without a lump. No label changes.
2. **No floor.** A state of M5.39 lies under every last stop on 65³ (by 3.0 × 10⁻⁴) and on 77³ (by 5.3 × 10⁻⁴); both were above the gate. The `iso` routes were still falling at their last stop. On 55³ the routes split in two groups by their lowest center eigenvalue: `smooth` and your field (0.330) end 1.15 and 1.18 × 10⁻³ under `chain` and `iso` (0.346, 0.349), and differ from them by 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ (relative) in the inertia. On 65³ and 77³ every route that meets the rule is in the upper group (0.341 to 0.345), and the lower states of M5.39 are in the other (0.332, 0.329). Whether the upper group is a second valley or a slow descent is not settled.

3. Inertia and g_A e² fixed on 45³, 55³, 65³: not met for the inertia on 45³ `iso` and 65³ `iso` (1.1 and 1.3 × 10⁻⁴), and for all three readouts on 65³ `smooth`, which holds lumps.
4. Half range between routes: met for the inertia and g_A e²; for μ_V the recorded 3 × 10⁻⁴ appears on 55³ only.
5. Intercepts within half a percent of the trend of M5.40: met (35.914, 16.283, script μ_V 3.1484, e 4.2221, f_π 93.21, g_A 0.9134). The second half, a route range at least an order under the distance from the 55³ value to the intercept, holds for the inertia, g_A e², the script's μ_V and g_A (81 to 1370 times) and not for μ_V at the gap Δ (4 times).
