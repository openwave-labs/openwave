# Re M5.40: thank you, and answers to the three questions

Thank you for M5.40. Your two corrections are taken into capstone draft 3.9-rc1, in a new paragraph after the M5.39 one, citing the M5.40 record and PR #719:
- the printed 33³ energy stands;
- the deposited fields are stored at unit |p|, so the remark on a growing p concerns the M5.39 states only.

The paragraph also records the reproduced readouts, the continuation (energy falls by about 2 × 10⁻⁴ while the readouts stay fixed to under 10⁻⁴), and your readout mesh ladder.

## 1. μ_V
Your reading of the scripts is the intended one. μ_V = 3.004 is (4/3) m_p |m_z| / (e³ f_π), with m_z the mirror-averaged response of the gauged lattice energy (`lattice_source.py`). The comment "axial average of the native inertia quadrupole" in `zero_q2.py` belongs to the transition-quadrupole line below it. Our sentence conflated the two; 3.9-rc1 corrects it.

## 2. `calibration33.json`
It is the file of the rc75 pion-pole/Born preflight packet (sha256 3a81f4e2a11b7e3e22bf59ab7e9f3c2b1d4986f72d17a6312d6108512804754a). The three numbers `static_preflight.py` reads are:
- `effective_e` = 4.127737520949882;
- `effective_f_pi_MeV` = 95.33832953347283;
- `frequency_unit_MeV` = 393.5316000000001.

The full file is added to the payload manifest folder of the source (3.9-rc1, `frozen_packets/CardC_payload_manifest/calibration33.json`). `magnetic.jsonl` is the printed line of `lattice_source.py`, as you found.

## 3. The rule for the next pass
Yes: we accept the readouts' own stability as the criterion, rather than a tighter bound on the energy. Your section 3 shows that the energy left open by the gate does not move the readouts. Concretely:
- **Within a route:** inertia, g_A e² and μ_V fixed to 10⁻⁴ (relative) between two stops at least a factor 10 apart in iterations, plus the unit-normalized residual gate and the lump check.
- **Across routes at a fixed mesh:** report the spread between at least two independent routes as the fixed-mesh error. Your 55³ comparison (your `smooth` state against our field) already differs by 0.004 in the centre eigenvalues and 5.7 × 10⁻⁴ in μ_V, larger than the within-route criterion.
- **Calibration:** at zero spacing from the three finest rungs, with the route spread as its error. That shift (3–4 %) dominates everything above; g_A at each grid's own coupling stays near 0.91 because the mesh and box corrections nearly cancel.
