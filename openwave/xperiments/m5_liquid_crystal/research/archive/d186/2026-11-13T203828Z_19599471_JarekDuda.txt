What v3.5 adds

  - core-localized inertia χ(M) opens the charge core's minor gap toward 0.2, but not for the electron: a Compton clock has E_rot = m_e c²/4, which raises a LEP2-sized core's gap by only 7 %;
  - a clock region a few Compton lengths across, held by line tension, has E = 2JΩ = ħΩ at J = ħ/2;
  - general virial E/JΩ = 2 + Σ(1−p)E_p/2E_rot;
  - spin selection is open.

- New section on the time axis as an SO(1,3) or SO(4) vacuum (pp. 25–30):
  - 1+3 is generic for a 4D Landau–de Gennes tensor (its cubic invariant); 2+2 needs strong order;
  - a positive Boltzmann weight of an action cannot carry a light cone; one of the squared field equation can;
  - a compact Euclidean world forces singular points of the time axis: a bang and a crunch.

Credit: NovaraX (Rafał Wiktorowicz) computed our neutrino-loop action card v1. The card was frozen before the run, the predictions were recorded beforehand, and the 1 % rule was applied. Main results come from at least two independent codes and an adversarial check:
- Held axis: the leading log (π/2)g_k² is reproduced by three codes (fit C = 1.5705, p = 1.0000 ± 0.0002). This checks the numerics, since p = 1 follows from the action on that branch.
- The flat class-3 core is a saddle for every δ in (0, 1/2). Free, it decays into classes 1 + 2 with the ratio δ² + (1−δ)².
- A single precessing core exists in 2D only with a constraint, and its ratio depends on the constraint. Our five precession predictions are therefore unresolved, not failed.
- The card's equation does not select δ. There is an exact duality E_δ[M] = E_{1−δ}[1 − M], and each breaker of it (tr M³, or κ tr(M∂M∂M)) adds one coefficient.
- Consequence for R25c: its 115 meV branch needs a hold that the action does not yet provide; without one the same card gives 170 meV.
