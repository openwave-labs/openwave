## S1. Lepton spectrum: one charge, three states (row: lepton masses ×)

**Why.** The axis-permutation reading (three hedgehogs, one per axis) gave 1 : 5.9 : 15.1 and is closed: the three assignments carry different charges. The surviving construction keeps one long-axis hedgehog with Q = −1 and looks for internal excitations of its core. Its biaxial profile ε(r) → 0 outside.

**Space.**
- The V4 coefficients (w, C_p), plus a sextic potential term. A sextic is the lowest order that can reach the Koide condition, written as a biaxial order-parameter identity Tr U² = Tr V².
- The weights of the selective and spectral kinetic terms.
- The toy point (s, g, δ).
- Keep fixed what the table already relies on: exact ±e²/d outside the cores, Λ_own = 0, and the D8 gap.

**States.** For each parameter point, the lowest three stationary Q = −1 solutions on your electron-ID instrument, with the clock running. Count excitations of the core (transverse-string arrangement, clock harmonic, radial node), not other charges.

**Observable.** Rest energies E₁ ≤ E₂ ≤ E₃ (inertial mass checked against E), the ratios E₂/E₁ and E₃/E₁, and Koide's Q = Σm/(Σ√m)².

**Gates.**
- *Green candidate:* ratios within 10% of 206.77 and 3477.2, and Q within 1% of 2/3, at a point that passes the fixed rows.
- *Informative null:* the map of the largest reachable E₂/E₁ over the space. If it stays below about 20, the row stays × with a bound, and the bound says which term would have to supply the rest.

**Our prediction (uncertain).** Polynomial potentials alone stay within one order of magnitude. Large ratios need the conflict between the core and the surrounding twisting clock field.

**Cost.** One stationary 3D solve per state and point; a few thousand points with adaptive sampling.

## S2. Quadratic record dynamics: ghosts, spin 2, c_T = c (rows: gravity, Lorentz)

**Why.** The record commutator starts at fourth order, so gravitational waves need a quadratic record kinetic term. Rule-R gravity also needs a comoving u (|α₁ − 2α₂| = 8 otherwise).

**Space.** Coefficients of the candidate quadratic terms around the vacuum M = diag(g, 1, δ, 0):
- K_T, K_cov, K_h, I1_h and the spectral kinetic term (your forms);
- a record torsion term T = ∂_λE_Aα − ∂_αE_Aλ, for the single-field reading.

**Observable.** Plane-wave spectrum of all 16 components:
- the kinetic norm of each mode (your D8 criterion);
- dispersion relations, mass gaps and helicity content;
- whether a massless helicity-±2 pair propagates with ω = ck.

**Gates.**
- *Success:* a region with no negative norm on any propagating mode, a massless spin-2 pair with c_T = c exactly in the continuum limit (GW170817 allows |c_T/c − 1| ≲ 10⁻¹⁵), and every other mode gapped or pure gauge.
- *Null:* a map of which ghost or extra massless mode blocks it, per term.

**Our prediction (uncertain).** The ghost-free region exists only for the TEGR-like torsion combination.

**Cost.** Linear algebra over (k, coefficients): cheap per point, with a large scan.
