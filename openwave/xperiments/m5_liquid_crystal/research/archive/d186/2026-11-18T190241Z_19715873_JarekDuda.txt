**2. Why the start decides. The algebra is S; that it explains your spread is C.**
- **One generator costs nothing.** With the eigenvalues held by the potential, ∂ᵢM = [Ωᵢ, M], and
  [∂ᵢM, ∂ⱼM] = B_M([Ωᵢ, Ωⱼ]). B_M multiplies each entry of the bracket by a product of two gaps. Take eigenvalues
  1, 1+δ, 2:
  - the bracket in the minor plane gets (1−δ);
  - the two brackets that mix the major axis with the minor pair get δ and δ(1−δ).

  So at δ = 0.05 two of the three channels are about 360 times softer in the quartic (panel b). A field with only one
  generator, Ωᵢ = θᵢA, costs exactly zero, whatever its profile θ(x).
- **The inertia is not blind.** Its density is |∇θ|²·|[K,[A,M]]|², which grows without bound as the profile steepens.
  `flat_family_check.py` gives a commutator of 9 × 10⁻¹⁴ at slope 50, while the inertia density grows 10⁴-fold for a
  100-fold slope.
- **So the energy cannot pick the state.** Along these directions U is flat or nearly flat, while I has no bound. The
  grid and the start pick it. This fits your numbers: U within 1–3 % across the states of one grid, I spread by a
  factor 1.2–1.7.
- **This is known in variational problems that do not control the gradient.** Minimizing sequences refine without limit
  (Ball and James 1987), and a small gradient term then sets the scale (Kohn and Müller 1992). Our estimate from
  18 Nov, 02:27, wall width ≈ 15√L₁, is that balance (C).
- **Every 2-form energy misses these fields.** Both the commutator and the Gram/Skyrme-type quartic vanish on a gradient
  built from one generator (S). The terms at most quadratic in time derivatives are the σ₁, σ₂ and σ₃ terms (Adam,
  Sánchez-Guillén and Wereszczyński 2010), and of these only σ₁, the L₁ term, sees such fields (C for the class
  statement). This is why "bound commuting gradients without L₁'s cost" has no cheap answer.
- **The same structure appears in the founding (2108) action:** Sol's flat directions in capstone v3.98, App. E(k).

**3. What scales (C).** I·h is 793, 1021 and 984 for the lowest-U state on each grid, and 594, 786 and 843 for the states
from the seed. That is compatible with I ∝ 1/h for walls of fixed amplitude and width h: h∫‖∇M‖² is about 435–490 in
both codes. We read no exponent from it, as you don't.
