## 1. Your registered bands: M5.49

**What was run.** The relaxed unit of the M5.42 action at $J = 0$, on half-boxes 8, 10 and 13 at spacing 0.25 (half-box 13 was added so that both of your rays reach their edges at $\omega = 5.5$). Free noise in the mid polarization was evolved with the time part of M5.47 and read on pieces of 0.5 along four lines through the center of the loop: the axis, the ring-plane ray along a box axis (your ray), the ring-plane diagonal, and one oblique line. On each piece a cutoff was fitted; a band is a run of pieces whose cutoff lies under $\omega$, at least half a local wavelength long. Our own prediction of the bands, your rule $m_{\mathrm{eff}}^2 = L_1 m^2 / I(r)$ applied to the background relaxed on OpenWave, was written to a file and hashed before any wave was read along a ray. Only half-box 13 is new data: the noise runs of half-boxes 8 and 10 are mostly those of M5.47, which had read them in bins of the kinetic factor, and the order of prediction and read is shown by local file times and logs.

**What stands behind the words.**

- The read edges follow the cutoffs that M5.47 had fitted per bin of the kinetic factor (within 1.4 %), which sit a few percent under $\sqrt{m^2 / \bar q}$. That offset moves each outer edge outward by 3.5 to 7.4 % against our own prediction, and it stays inside your tolerance. The held words therefore hold with that offset in them.
- On the axis at $\omega = 4$ the background relaxed on OpenWave sits at the margin of your statement: the least $m_{\mathrm{eff}}^2$ on the axis is 15.50, 16.08 and 16.38 on half-boxes 8, 10 and 13 when averaged on our pieces (14.92, 15.52 and 15.82 on the ray itself), against your 16.58 and against $\omega^2 = 16$. The band read on half-box 8 rests on that one box, and on our plan's choice of the piece that sets the half wavelength.
- Our prediction's outer edges lie within 2.3 % of your registered ones. Its inner edges lie 2 to 5 % under yours in the ring plane and 24 to 29 % under on the axis at $\omega = 5.5$.
- Your kinetic factor uses the direction of the mode with three weights; ours uses the mid polarization of M5.47. The predicted edges under the two differ by at most 1.5 %.
- Two controls were met only in part, with no word changed: three statements at $\omega = 4$ (two of them bands) were not repeated by a second noise draw (the scored lines among them are scored on edges, which the second draw finds again within 0.7 %), and the read inner edges of half-box 8 do not separate between lines as the prediction does.

## 2. The ladder with the separation held: M5.50

**What was run.** The relaxed unit, with the faces held, under a penalty that holds the size of the loop: $E_\mu = U + \tfrac{\mu}{2} (D[M] - d)^2$, where $D$ is twice the mean distance of the squared potential density from its own center and $d$ is raised in steps; the force of the hold is $f = \mu (d - D)$. Half-boxes 10 and 14 at spacings 0.5 and 0.25, targets up to 13 and 20.5, a family of perturbations that seek a split, and the four free-faced exits of M5.46 run again with a read at every iteration near the exit: 152 chains.

**No label.** The control on the two new base states of half-box 14 asked that the radius of the loop agree with half-box 10 within 0.03. Our reader gives gaps of 0.096 and 0.045. That reader takes the mean radius of the pierced plaquettes and moves in jumps larger than the bound; by two continuous measures the difference is 0.021 to 0.024. The control stays not met as it was worded before the run, and by the plan every label of the ladder is withheld. What follows is what the frozen rules give on the chains as run, and none of it is a label.

- **The cost of the separation rose.** On every grid $U$ rose from each held rung to the next, and $f$ grew with the separation, reaching 1.06 at 13 (half-box 10) and 1.08 at 15.25 (half-box 14) at spacing 0.5. At spacing 0.25 this was read only up to 10. The two boxes disagree on the rise of $U$ by 6 to 14 % at every shared target, so part of the growth of $f$ is the faces.
- **No state ended as two lines**, on any chain, under the hold or in the family.
- **The hold was met without holding the line.** From a target of 16 on half-box 14 (spacing 0.5), on the rungs of half-box 14 at spacing 0.25 from 14.5 (cut at their wall caps in that state), on the continuation chains from lower targets, and on every pinch and the larger noise of the family, the loop went back to about its free size while one to three single nodes far from the vacuum carried the held quantity, at a lower $U$ than the stretched loop. There the ladder reads nothing about the line. Counted with the continuation chains or the capped rungs, this meets the instrument failure that our plan defines, and the second try the plan calls for was not run. A moment of the potential density is the wrong hold for this question; a later ladder needs one that follows the line itself.
- With held faces the whole number around a single line reads 1 by construction, so the ladder's informative reads are the cost and the number of lines, not the charge.

**The exits, part (c) of your corrected clause.** This reading keeps its label, since these chains do not start from the new bases. On three of the four exits (half-box 8 at both spacings, and half-box 10 at spacing 0.25), between the last read of 1 and the first read of 0 on the box surface there are one or two iterations at which that surface is not liftable while no pierced plaquette is left anywhere in the box. By the rule frozen before the run, that is not held. A descriptor added during the run places those reads: the eigenvalue gap closes under 0.2 on 2 to 22 nodes of the face the loop has just left, within 0.5 of the center of its last piece. The fourth exit (half-box 10, spacing 0.5) reads held. No surface on any chain read ½; on a liftable surface a whole value holds by construction.
