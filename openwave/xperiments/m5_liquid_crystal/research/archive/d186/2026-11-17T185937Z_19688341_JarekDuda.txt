# Post for OpenWave discussion #186, reply to M5.42 (draft for Jarek)

Before posting, insert the Zenodo link and attach `fig_ring_17nov.png`.

**On M5.42.** Thank you, Rodrigo, Fable and the auditors. It is the clearest negative result the clock line has had.

1. **The 0.83 is withdrawn as evidence.**
   - M5.42 shows that the state behind it is out of configuration, with a lower state beside it.
   - Its ratio is set by the box: 0.839, 0.317, 0.129 and 0.031 on half-boxes 6 to 18 at J_e/10.
   - Capstone 3.97, deposited today, corrects the text and cites M5.42: https://zenodo.org/records/24322890 .
2. **What the degree-0 object is** (figure attached).
   - The gap between the two largest eigenvalues closes on a closed loop. This is a half-turn ring of the long axis,
     uniform inside and radial outside.
   - It sits at 0.69–0.71 of the half-box: radius 3.11, 4.25 and 6.13 on half-boxes 4.5, 6 and 9, the same at three
     spacings.
   - It is where your spheres lose the degree (gap 0.008 at r = 5) and where your one-cell states form (r ≈ 4.5 near
     z = 0).
   - Your two symmetry sectors are two orientations of this ring: around the equator (symmetric) or standing on a face
     diagonal (broken). We get ΔU = 0.308 and ΔI = 16.5 (your 16.4).
   - Your free-face run fits this picture: only the faces hold the ring, so without them it leaves.
3. **The twist about r̂ is not a symmetry of the ring.**
   - A rigid turn of 0.3 rad costs about 6.
   - So E = U + J²/2I with this twist is a functional, not the energy of a stationary rotation. That is the other side of
     your box law.
4. **Our recorded predictions, scored by your record.**
   - **Held:**
     - the one-cell amplitude grows as J^{1/4} (10^{1/4} = 1.78; you read 1.64–1.91);
     - the L₁ = 0 fall to 0.35 was the stencil (7.65 on the mirror-averaged one);
     - the band only caps the spike (7 nodes at its edge).
   - **Mostly held:** the spike's inertia grows as 1/h. Your exponents are −0.69 to −0.85, with three of four reads in
     the window.
   - **Failed:** the one-cell energy falling linearly with the spacing (you read exponents 0.51–0.76). The floor we expect,
     about 4, would not show yet.
   - **Not tested:** L₁ = 0.
5. **Your δ = 0.05 read is the encouraging one.**
   - The inertia inside r < 2 is about the same on both boxes, 521 and 547 (the second not converged). That is a quarter
     and a fifth of the whole, against under 1 % at δ = 0.3.
   - This is what δ_ext → 0 should give, since the twist outside becomes gauge.
   - Two reads on your saved end states would test it without a new relaxation:
     - the long axis's degree on r = 3 and 4 at δ = 0.05 (is the one state with a nonzero degree on r = 4 there, and
       does its core carry the inertia?);
     - the ring radius on half-boxes 12 and 18 at δ = 0.3.
6. **Open: what fixes the ring's size.**
   - In the same action a straight half-turn line has a finite core and a logarithmic far field, with coefficient
     2πL₁·2(1 − δ)²/4. So L₁ does resist growth.
   - But at δ = 0.1 the ring still grows with the box (3.87 and 5.44 on half-boxes 6 and 9), against a predicted size of
     about 4.
