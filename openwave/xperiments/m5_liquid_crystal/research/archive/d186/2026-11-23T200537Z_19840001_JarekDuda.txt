What changed for the M5 rows

The fixed-J clock fails with a free exterior (S(Sol); the crossing checked, S).
Take a twist at conserved J about the major axis, with a uniaxial exterior that is not pinned. A shell distortion that keeps the degree and stays on the vacuum orbit changes the fixed-J energy by ΔU − Ω²ΔI/2, with ΔU/ε² = 41.0/R + 44.7/R⁵ and ΔI/ε² = 15.0R³ + 13.3R + 5.5/R³.
For any Ω ≠ 0, an outer shell lowers the energy. At J = I₀ = 0.5 the crossing is at R = 1.469.
This is the infrared wall of R4–R12 in another form: a free exterior absorbs the clock charge.
(C) #190's exterior-degenerate SO(2) fixes an aligned exterior NNᵀ, but not the radial director around a charge. So for a charge, the protection has to come from a pinned or gauged exterior.
No clock driven by terms quartic in velocities (S, symbolic).
For L = av² + bv⁴, H = av² + 3bv⁴, but the Euclidean action of the 4D ensemble is S_E = av_E² − bv_E⁴. No sign of b bounds both. This covers the (F·F)² clock of R11.
Stabilizers quadratic in velocities, e.g. σ₁XY − σ₂Y², give H = S_E ≥ 0. OpenWave's time part T (M5.47) is of this allowed kind.
Spin ½ is not from the tensor alone (S, a theorem in v4.3). In a bosonic theory whose Hamiltonian is even in the momenta, every half-integer-spin level comes doubled. So the electron is the Dirac field in frame variables (v4.2, with g = 2). The clock row at J = ħ/2 is therefore a coupled tensor–Dirac target.

An exterior twist radiates: δ_ext ≤ 4.5 × 10⁻¹⁹ is needed to survive 13.8 Gyr (S(Sol)).
The gravity frame is TEGR plus a khronon on the luminal ray, c_T = c_S = 1 (C).

Four rungs, if OpenWave or Substrate have cycles. Predictions were registered on 23 Nov at 20:53 CET, before any run. My confidence is in brackets.

T1, the shell test on a fixed-J clock (suited to Substrate's #190).

Setup: a relaxed charged defect (point hedgehog, or the v4.1 ring) at fixed J.
Add a shell distortion of amplitude ε at radius R that keeps the degree and stays on the vacuum orbit.
Read ΔE_J = ΔU − Ω²ΔI/2 against R, up to the box.
Run the same test with an aligned exterior NNᵀ as the control.
Prediction (C) [70 %]: around the charge, ΔI grows with R (as R³ in Sol's chart), and an outer crossing R* exists where ΔE_J < 0. With the aligned exterior, ΔI stays bounded and there is no crossing.
Falsifier: no crossing up to the box around the charge. That would mean #190's mechanism carries over to charges.

T3, the charge ladder with the line pinned (the M5.50 follow-up, on the M5.42 action).

Setup:
Impose the field of a ½ line as Dirichlet data on two thin cylinders at x = ±d/2, d = 6 to 20, and relax the rest.
Read U(d), and F = −dU/dd as the force.
Measure the tension of a straight ½ line in the same action and box.
Prediction (C) [60 %]: U(d) is linear for d above about three loop radii, with slope within 15 % of the straight-line tension. No split.
