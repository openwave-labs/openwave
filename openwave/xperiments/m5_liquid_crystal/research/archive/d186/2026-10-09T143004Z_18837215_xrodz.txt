# Direction poll for the next rung: nine computations your own notes name as missing, in your words, for you to rank

## TL;DR

1. A question before the next rung: which of the nine computations below should come first? Rank them (a top three is enough), say which inputs you can supply (scripts, normalizations, saved endpoint fields), and name the verdict criterion where the text leaves it open. The next rung is the top-ranked row; R27's two open questions (the size of kappa, what holds the bound {2,2}) stay on the table independently.
2. The nine are lifted from your own texts: the 09-29 proton-stability note here (P-STAB-1 and its "please attack" list), the 10-09 records on nuclear structure ([23256071](https://zenodo.org/records/23256071), v4.6.4) and on renormalization before second quantization ([23263153](https://zenodo.org/records/23263153), v3.2), report 018 and its walls appendix, report 017's uncomputed gate, and P249/P250. Each row says what the text calls missing, what we would run, and what we would need from you.
3. Two of the nine carry physical units on their own (D1 in fm and hbar, D3 in fm against the helium bound); the rest stay dimensionless at whatever (g, delta) their source uses. We are not claiming to reach g 1e10, delta 1e-10; a row that needs the physical point says so.
4. Every deliverable follows the standard the nuclear record sets for outside numerics: raw fields, optimizer histories, mesh and box scans, scripts pinned to a commit, a fresh-agent audit with its own code. Endpoint energies alone would not count, and we agree.
