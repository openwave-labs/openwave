**On R32** ([[record](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/tasks/m5_32_task_details.md)](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/tasks/m5_32_task_details.md)): thank you for the strand diagnostic, the independent reproduction of the anchor, and the updated lepton cell.

- **"The host is mostly removed, and the jumps follow the host": agreed.** It fits what Release 2.3 now says.
  - The five-strand Z₃ shell is a static saddle. The channel triplet has to come from the Dirac operator on a relaxed core, not from strand lines.
  - The capstone's criterion closes the classical route anyway. A core below 2.8×10⁻⁴ fm has natural energy ħc/r ≈ 0.7 TeV, so the electron would have to sit at about 7×10⁻⁷ of it. A Dirac electron binds to a core it empties only beyond 450 fm.
  - So no S-run until a relaxed host binds Dirac states, as you say. When one does, L28 now gives the target it must hit: at m₁ = 0, TM1 needs seesaw columns that annihilate (j + k)/√2 in the channel basis.
- **The W ratio.** The closed form is W_μ/W_e − 1 = −(δ_μ − δ_e)/2, which equals (κ_h,μ − κ_h,e)/4, with δᵢ = (b/M)² rᵢ²/(1 + rᵢ²)³. It is printed in Sec. 5 since Release 2.2 and computed by L24. It matches the exact SVD to six digits at both points:
  - LB1: −5.718×10⁻¹⁰.
  - Release 2.0 baseline (M = 10 TeV, b = 200 GeV, r_e = 0.0015984, r_μ = 0.022991): −0.10504 ppm.
  
  A factor-200 miss at the baseline most likely means the baseline's own amplitudes weren't used.
- **The dipole normalization.** Your factor 4.36 is exactly e√(m_μ/m_e). The μ → eγ form uses the generic off-diagonal chirality factor, d_eμ = c_d √(m_e m_μ)/(16π² M²), with e kept in the rate, Γ = α m³(|d_L|² + |d_R|²)/4. The EDM form, d_e = e m_e c_d/(16π² M²), is the diagonal one. Both are stated in Sec. 12 (Release 2.2 onward, L24).
- **The lepton cell.** Thank you; the ⚠️ is fair for the criterion as you read it. When convenient, please point it to Release 2.3. Its Dirac–core coupling is now CPT-even, and the old pseudoscalar twist coupling was CPT-odd.

**2. Leptons, Release 2.3.**
