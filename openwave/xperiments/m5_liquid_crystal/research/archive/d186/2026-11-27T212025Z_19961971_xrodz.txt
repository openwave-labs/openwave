## 3. What the stage-0 read is, on a real tensor

The fields on OpenWave are real and symmetric: the $4 \times 4$ tensor $M$ in runs of the lepton series, and a $3 \times 3$ field, with no time-like eigenvector, in the ring states of M5.51, M5.56 and M5.57 and in the baryon analogs of M5.22. Where its eigenvalue is not degenerate, an eigenvector of a real symmetric tensor is real and defined up to a sign, so its Berry connection vanishes and only a sign holonomy is left. In capstone 4.8 the charge is a Chern-1 hedgehog of the time line in the $(t, p)$ plane (SL-1), and the document keeps the complex coframe apart from the real one and from the tensor $M$. The charge instrument on record on OpenWave ([`m5_theory_canonical.md`](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/m5_theory_canonical.md)) is the dual curvature of the oriented long axis $\hat n$:

```math
E_k = \frac{1}{2} \epsilon_{kij} \hat n \cdot \left( \partial_i \hat n \times \partial_j \hat n \right)
```

It is Coulomb on the hedgehog, and its flux over $4 \pi$ reads $\pm 1$ on every charged state of the record. Where $\hat n$ is smooth its divergence vanishes, so as a charge density it sits only where the long axis is degenerate (the cores and the lines).

1. **Which density do you mean on our states?** (a) The instrument above on the neutral states: whether its charge, zero in total, is spread over separate cores and lines. (b) A complex line built from the time-like eigenvector of the $4 \times 4$ tensor and the spatial line $p$ of SL-1: then which eigenvector of ours is $p$, and with which phase. (c) A lift of a stored $M$ to the complex coframe $E$: then the map from $M$ to $E$.
2. **Which states count?** On record: single rings and pairs of rings ($3 \times 3$, static; the far field of the pair has degree 2); the baryon analogs of [M5.22](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/tasks/m5_22_task_details.md), a $3 \times 3$ census with a neutral state ($Q = 0$, a column and two rings) beside a charged one; and the states of the lepton series, one of them carried at fixed angular momentum (M5.21.9). Which of these is a neutral $B = 1$ state in your sense?
3. **"Rotating with the frame."** The reading under FB-7 takes the proton and the neutron as two isospin states of one rotating core. Is the read then on a state carried at fixed rotation, or is it the change of the density under a rigid rotation of a static state?

With (a) the read is a fixed-field read on existing states and its plan is short. With (b) or (c) the plan starts from your definition, with a known-answer control on the hedgehog.
