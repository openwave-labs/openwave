# M5.40 on the Card C payload: your fields reproduce their records and the printed readouts

## TL;DR

1. Thank you for v3.7 and the payload. All 14 digests verify, and the OpenWave evaluator returns the recorded energies of the 33³ and 55³ fields to 12 digits.
2. The printed inertias, center eigenvalues, gap, radii, μ_V and the three axial values are reproduced from your fields by code written from the deposited definitions.
3. One correction to our last post: your fields are stored at unit |p|, so the remark that the gate is read on a vector whose length grows describes the states of M5.39 only, not yours.
4. Every 33³ state of M5.39 lies above your field. The failed 33³ control of M5.39 was our routes stopping high; your printed energy stands.
5. Continued under the card's optimizer, each field falls by about 2 × 10⁻⁴, while the inertia, the radii and g_A e² move by under 5 × 10⁻⁵.
6. Two of the deposited scripts stop on a missing input, and we have three questions (section 6).

Record on OpenWave: [M5.40](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/tasks/m5_40_task_details.md) (§ 9 to § 16), merged in [PR #719](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/pull/719); every number below is in [`m5_40_summary.json`](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/data/m5_40_summary.json).

## 4. The readout definitions, as we read them in the scripts

With q = p / |p|, a = τ(q) f, N = − q qᵀ + a aᵀ / G_time, E_N the tensor quadratic and E_4 the quaternion quartic:

```text
zero_q2.py, baryon_moments: on the 24 simplices of a cell, at the four rule points P (vertices v_0..v_3
along the path, D_m = (v_m - v_{m-1}) / h, o the orientation of the path):
    rho(P) = - o det[P; D_1; D_2; D_3] / |P|^4          weight h^3 / (192 pi^2)
    r_S^2 = l0^2 ( sum rho |x|^2 / B - |x_c|^2 ),   B = sum rho,   x_c = sum rho x / B

zero_q2.py, iso_moments: on cell midpoints, delta_a q = T_a q, delta_a N = [T_a, N]:
    lambda_ab = (1/2) (delta_a N : delta_b N)
              + sum_i [ (delta_a q . delta_b q) |d_i q|^2 - (d_i q . delta_a q)(d_i q . delta_b q) ]
    Lambda = h^3 sum lambda,   M = h^3 sum lambda |x - x_c|^2
    r_V^2 = l0^2 tr M / tr Lambda,   gap = 3 e^3 f_pi / (2 l),   l = tr Lambda / 3

lattice_source.py: every link x -> y carries phi = (1/2) (x cross y) . B; the far end is rotated by phi
in the (q_1, q_2) plane (N conjugated); E[B] = E_N + E_4, averaged over the lattice and its three mirrors
    m_z = dE / dB_z at 0 (central difference, step 5e-5)
    mu_V = (4/3) m_p |m_z| / (e^3 f_pi)        rigid: (2/3) m_p l / (e^3 f_pi) = m_p / gap

static_preflight.py, axial_twist: U_ai(eps) = rotation by eps x_i in the (q_0, q_a) plane,
q -> U q, N -> U N U^T; f, b(q_0), c(q_0) and every other term held
    D^N_ai = dE_N / d eps,   D^4_ai = dE_4 / d eps   (central difference, step 5e-5)
    g_A = ( 2 / (9 e^2) ) | sum_ai R_ai ( D^N_ai + D^4_ai ) |
    R = the orthogonal matrix nearest to the fit of (q_1, q_2, q_3) on the lattice dipole fields, 2.5 <= r < 2.8

constants: e = 4.259, f_pi = 92.4 MeV, m_p = 938.272 MeV, l0 = hbar c / (e f_pi)
```

## 6. Three questions

1. **μ_V.** The README and your reply say the printed 3.004 includes the axial average of the native inertia quadrupole. In the deposited `zero_q2.py` the printed moment is (4/3) m_p |m_z| / (e³ f_π) with m_z from `lattice_source.py`, and the quadrupole Q enters only the transition quadrupole. The number is reproduced either way; which description is the intended one?
2. **`calibration33.json`.** Could its three numbers be added to the record? Only e enters the axial value, and the tail couplings of `static_preflight.py` need all three.
3. **The rule for the next pass.** A gate on the unit-normalized residual plus a lump check is what your own fields already meet, and section 3 shows that neither that gate nor a small drift certifies a floor at the 10⁻⁴ level in the energy. For the converged background, would you accept the readouts' own stability as the criterion (the inertia fixed to 10⁻⁴ between two stops), or do you want a tighter bound on the energy itself?
