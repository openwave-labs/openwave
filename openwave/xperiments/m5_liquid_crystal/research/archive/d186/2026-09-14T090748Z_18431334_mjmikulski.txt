@xrodz — the L-ladder is complete; the appendix to 008 with all three boxes is in [PR #25](https://github.com/mjmikulski/the-final-lagrangian-of-physics/pull/25) ([appendix text](https://github.com/mjmikulski/the-final-lagrangian-of-physics/blob/appendix/008-L-ladder/reports/008-i1-squared-clock/APPENDIX-L-ladder.md), [figure](https://github.com/mjmikulski/the-final-lagrangian-of-physics/blob/appendix/008-L-ladder/reports/008-i1-squared-clock/results/L_ladder/fig_L_ladder.png)).

| L (N) | E_stat | frozen-profile ω_E | sampled minimum (all 5 protocol levels) | depth vs ω = 0 | depth plateau |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 48 (32) | 4.835 | 0.326 | 0.35 | 6.6·10⁻⁵ | yes |
| 72 (48) | 4.548 | 0.280 | 0.2 | 6.7·10⁻⁵ | no (8.3 → 6.7·10⁻⁵) |
| 96 (64) | 4.377 | 0.249 | 0.2 | 2.5·10⁻⁴ | no (1.0 → 2.5·10⁻⁴, growing) |

So: the well survives the larger boxes; its location moves one rung down from L = 48 to 72, following the frozen-profile prediction, and then stays at the rung resolution from 72 to 96; its depth is converged only in the report's box — in the larger boxes 008's fixed-depth protocol (500 Adam + four L-BFGS cycles) resolves the bracket but not the 10⁻⁴-level energy differences (rung residuals 10⁻², ten times the report's), so the L = 96 depth is a lower bound under that protocol, not a value. Not done here: protocol deepening, continuum extrapolation, a per-box γ. Reproduction: `bash reproduce_appendix_L.sh` checks the committed records on CPU; `M5_RUN_LADDER=1` regenerates seeds and ladders (about a GPU-day).
