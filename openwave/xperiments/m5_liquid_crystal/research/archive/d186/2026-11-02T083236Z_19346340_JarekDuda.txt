The degenerate catalog (E₂/E₁ ≤ 1.03, E₃/E₁ ≤ 1.08, Q ≈ 1/3, stable under the second box) is that statement on your instrument. It closes S1 for classical stationary energies in the fixed-J frame.

2. **One request for the M5 lepton cell: keep ⚠️, but update its text.** The cell currently ends with "the pre-registered extrapolation bridge to physical parameters failed terminally (2026-08-07), leaving no live calibration route". Release 2.1 supplies a live calibration route of the kind your table credits elsewhere: parameters fixed by data, with a held-out check.
   - **Anchor (Sec. 11).** In the working reading, the masses sit in the coupling of the elementary Dirac field to its core: a circulant of three core channels with scale s, ratio u/s and angle θ. The electron and muon fix s and θ. With Koide's u/s = 1/√2 imposed, the tau is held out and lands at 1776.969 MeV (+0.43σ, tree level).
   - **Protected realization (Secs. 5–6).** A vectorlike-lepton embedding with one Z₄ source per family gives a tree tau of 1776.963 MeV from the same two inputs. The photon charge is exact and the W/Z/H couplings are universal to 10⁻⁷.
   - **A concrete benchmark, LB1 (Sec. 12.1, new in 2.1).** M = 30 TeV, b = 10 GeV, f = 300 TeV.
     - It passes μ→eγ (MEG II), τ→ℓγ, the electron EDM (with real couplings), g−2, e/μ universality and the Higgs couplings, even with generic O(1) dipole coefficients in both chiralities.
     - The data bound the parameters; they do not fix them, so LB1 is a point in a window, not a fit.
     - Scalar loops make Koide a condition on the physical (on-shell) source amplitudes. That is one counted renormalization condition, like pole anchoring.
     - Conversion to pole masses is not yet computed.
     - A release script reruns the checks and asserts 29 stated numbers.
   - **What it is not.** It is not a derivation: three masses still need two inputs plus one structural assumption, as the Standard Model needs three Yukawas. The native origin of the circulant is open. Your S1 null now shows that origin is not in classical core energies, which is where Release 2.0 had already moved it.

   **Proposed cell text:** "⚠️ [partial: accommodated with an anchor; native origin open] Masses reached in the Dirac–core coupling: electron and muon fix the circulant, Koide imposed, tau held out at +0.43σ (tree level); benchmark LB1 passes lepton-flavour, EDM, g−2 and universality checks (Release 2.1, 10.5281/zenodo.23879623).
