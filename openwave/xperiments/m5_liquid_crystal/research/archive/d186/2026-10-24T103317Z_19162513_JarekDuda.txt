**LdGS scorecard v1.1: Vortex v3's clock, a time axis that is not a preferred frame, and nuclei**

Working paper with all checks attached: *LdGS against the OpenWave criteria* v1.1, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23615446. It has 12 pp., and scripts V1–V51 are embedded in the PDF and also in the zip. Independent adversarial reviews rechecked it; their corrections are included.

1. **Vortex v3.0-rc1 replicates (4.46%), but needs a fixed external clock.**
   - Its new spatial term carries energy but no inertia. So a moving nucleon's inertial mass would be 2E_tr = 334 MeV below its rest energy; Cs-clock bounds on the proton are at about 10⁻¹⁶.
   - The Lorentz-invariant form (∂q·∂q)³ needs no clock. Refitted with the ω coupling held in the NN range, it gives all 15 rows within 9.2% (10.3% with a consistent radius target), against 14.1% for v2.6. It also gives g_A = 1.158, τ_n = 1030 s (v2.6: 1125) and Γ_Δ = 84 MeV.
   - Cost: the merged ⁴He binds by 1128 MeV instead of 889.

2. **The record's time axis is an internal clock, not a frame.**
   - L = −4I1 − V4, and every other record term, is invariant under internal and spacetime boosts separately; this was checked term by term.
   - The vacuum's timelike eigenvector breaks only the internal ones. So defects are exact boosted solutions in the same vacuum (moving hedgehog: M_I = E₀).
   - This closes "boosted defects transforming covariantly" for matter, by symmetry.
   - The row stays !: rule-R gravity contracts u with ∂_μ, so there u must comove with matter (|α₁ − 2α₂| = 8 otherwise).

3. **Nuclei: ! [conditional], no longer ×.**
   - The classical soliton merges ⁴He (889 MeV; measured 28.3). Two-thirds of that is the usual Skyrme-type merging and one-third the shared scalar bag.
   - The sextic reads as ω exchange (g²/4π 10.2–14.7; NN 6–20). It already gives a soft core between rigid nucleons (+270 MeV at 0.5 fm), so the merging comes from deformation, not from a missing ω.
   - Nucleon rotors with an attraction fitted to B_d, a_s and r_s give ³H 8.6–9.2 MeV (8.48) and ⁴He 33–37 MeV (28.3), via SVM, which reproduces Malfliet–Tjon V.
   - Why amber: the failing approximation, a classical soliton with ω in its contact limit, fails for every Skyrme model, and two mainstream calculations repair it.
     - A dynamical ω gives loosely bound clusters (Gudnason–Speight, JHEP 2020).
     - Vibrational zero-point energy gives ⁴He 6.99 against 7.08 MeV per nucleon (Gudnason–Halcrow, PLB 2024; at F_π = 51.8 MeV).
   - Neither has been run for this functional yet. The kit for both (relaxed 3D B = 4 with a Proca ω; the B = 1 and B = 4 vibrational spectra) is in the zip.
