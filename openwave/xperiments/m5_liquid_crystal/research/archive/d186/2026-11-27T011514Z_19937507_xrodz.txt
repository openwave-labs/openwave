4. **The free pair on half-boxes 24 and 30: two rings on every end, no label.** The separations read 32.2 to 33.0 on half-box 24 and 40.4 and 40.7 on half-box 30, against 25.5 on half-box 18. No cell of [`MODELS.md`](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/MODELS.md) changes. ## 1. What ran

The action as ported in [M5.51](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/tasks/m5_51_task_details.md) with the midpoint weight, at $\delta = 0.05$, in closed boxes with the faces held at a far field of degree 2. The hold is the one of the plan you reviewed: the first moment of a smooth weight on the low-gap set of the line, with $g_0 = 0.2$, held by a quadratic penalty with $\mu = 20$:

```math
w = \exp\left( - g_{12}^2 / g_0^2 \right) , \qquad D'_\pm = \frac{\sum_\pm \lvert x \rvert w}{\sum_\pm w} , \qquad D' = D'_+ + D'_- , \qquad E_\mu = U + \frac{\mu}{2} \left( D' - d \right)^2
```

For the control on one ring the moment is the one-ring form $D'_1 = \sum x w / \sum w$.

## 2. The control of the hold

The plan's control C5 takes the relaxed single ring of M5.56 (radius 2.939, normal along $\hat y$), holds $D'_1$ at $a$ = 1.0 and 2.0 along $\hat x$, and asks that the ring's fitted center follow within 0.1.

| Read | $a$ = 1.0 | $a$ = 2.0 | Bound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The held moment $D'_1$ at the end | 0.99845 | 1.99637 | |
| The fitted center, x | 0.851 | 1.683 | within 0.1 of $a$: fails |
| The fitted center, y | −0.118 | 0.128 | within 0.1 of 0: fails |
| The radius | 2.9445 | 2.9412 | within 0.02 of 2.9391: holds |
| Energy above the free ring | 0.0139 | 0.0548 | under the moved copy's 0.161 and 0.700: holds |
| After release | back on the free ring | back on the free ring | holds |

- The hold moved its moment by the full amount and the fitted center by about 85 percent of it; the rest of the moment's shift is not carried by the center.
- The held state at 1.0 ended on an abnormal end of the optimizer after three restarts (gradient gate $3.4 \times 10^{-6}$), which our rule does not count as converged; the one at 2.0 converged. Both releases returned to the free ring's energy and center, with one clause of the last-step check not met (the moment still moving by $7.8 \times 10^{-4}$ and $2.2 \times 10^{-4}$ against $10^{-4}$). The control fails on the center whichever way these are read.
- The result is the same at two tries. The first try exposed a defect of our relax script (an end message of the optimizer was not recognized); after the fix the held states agree with the first try to $2 \times 10^{-7}$.
- The audit re-derived the failure with a separate fit (x = 0.851 and 1.708), checked the gradient of $D'_1$ against finite differences to $2.8 \times 10^{-10}$, and found both held states stationary. With that fit the y clause fails only narrowly (−0.103 and 0.109); the verdict rests on x.
- **A limit of our control.** It pushes the ring in its own plane. The rings of the pair lie with their normals along the separation axis, so on the ladders the hold would move each ring along its normal. The control does not say what the hold does in that geometry.
- One held chain on the pair had started beside the control (target one below the free pair's moment) and was stopped at iteration 3000 by the control's verdict. At that stop, unconverged, it held $D'$ = 24.535 with the fitted separation at 24.528. That is the state at which it was stopped, one target from its start and unconverged; it is not a read of the plan and decides nothing about the hold on the pair.

On half-box 24 both ends are converged by the rules as applied (section 6) and stable, but 0.276 apart in $s$ and $1.0 \times 10^{-4}$ relative apart in energy. On half-box 30 one start ended on an abnormal end of the optimizer after its restarts, which our rule does not count as converged, and the two ends are 0.291 apart in any case.
- **What the numbers are.** On these ends the separation is larger on the larger box, and so is the distance of the rings to the faces (5.24, about 7.5, about 9.7). These are descriptors: by the frozen rule the rung has no word, and the record does not say whether the separation stops or follows the box. The energies of different boxes are not compared: the box enters them.
- **A turned ring.** A2 and A5 hold one ring whose normal left the separation axis, and each lies below the coaxial end of its box (by 0.0042 and by 0.0164, the second on a chain that did not converge). The coaxial pair is therefore not the lowest state found on these boxes. On each of the two boxes one start ended coaxial and the other with a turned ring; the run does not resolve what decides it.

- The merged object ended on an abnormal end of the optimizer after three restarts (gradient gate $4.4 \times 10^{-6}$, its moment still moving by $2.4 \times 10^{-4}$), as one line fitted by a circle of radius 6.45, at energy 40.2254. Its perturbed re-relaxation converged and returned to the same energy within $5 \times 10^{-12}$ relative. By the frozen rule the reading "a local minimum of the action" has no label at 0.375: the reference itself did not end converged, although its stability check was met.

## 6. What this does not show, and the faults of our plan

Static states of one action at $\delta = 0.05$ in closed boxes up to half-box 30. Nothing is read about $E(d)$, a contact maximum, the merged end under a hold, a turned class at a held separation, or whether the free separation stops in a larger box. No exchange sign, spin or statistics is read.

The faults are our plan's, not the action's:

- the control of the hold tests a ring moved in its plane, not along its normal;
- the convergence rule gives no word to a chain whose optimizer ends abnormally after its restarts at a small gradient (A3, A5, the merged reference at 0.375 and one held row of the control, gates near $10^{-6}$);
- A1 and A4 count as converged only by restarts that the plan's sentence does not grant; no word depends on it;
- the list of rows and the noise seeds were written after the go, before any chain of the plan ran, where the plan says "fixed before the go";
- short smoke runs of the new scripts were made after the go on fields that are not rows of the plan; one was a one-ring hold whose log was read before the control ran (120 iterations, its center at 0.229 for a moment of 0.494). The record lists them, and no reading or choice used them;
- the queue left out one stability check the plan names (on the merged reference at 0.375); it ran before the close.

The record lists seventeen deviations. It was checked twice before this post: a recomputation with separate code of the energies and gradient gates of 13 stored states and of the $D'$ reader (the circle fit was not reimplemented there), and an adversarial audit in a fresh context (no blocker, four majors, eight minors, all folded). The builder of the record and its auditor were instances of one model family, in separate contexts. The fold was not audited again.

Record on OpenWave: the task document linked above; the scripts [`m5_57_hold.py`](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/scripts/m5_57_hold.py) (the moment, the hold and their controls), [`m5_57_relax.py`](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/scripts/m5_57_relax.py) (the chains) and [`m5_57_reads.py`](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/scripts/m5_57_reads.py) (the words and the summary); and [`m5_57_summary.json`](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/data/m5_57_summary.json).
