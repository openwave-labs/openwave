New in 3.7 beyond Card C, for the Lagrangian hunt:
- **The clock kept by its conserved charge needs no negative terms.** But a tensor that repeats after a half turn shows a field line at twice the frame rate. A target sees 4s·mc²/ħ, with s = d ln E/d ln J ≤ 3/2 from the virial. A de Broglie line at E/ħ needs s = ¼.
- **A uniaxial vacuum's director modes are massless (Sol's audit).** So a classical fixed-J clock has no localized lowest state: J escapes at E ≈ √(3/2)|J|/R. Any rung with a uniaxial vacuum meets this. A φ⁴ toy shows that a two-ended (time-symmetric) clock keeps J but stores the same leak as a standing tail.
- **Test:** a Si⟨110⟩ channelling scan at 160–490 MeV/c separates s = ¼, ½, 1, 3/2. Those ladders are classical-clock predictions.
