4. **The rule frozen on OpenWave before the run gives NEITHER.** It asked whether, between half-boxes 12 and 18, the radius changes by less than 5 percent (SATURATES) or the radius over the half-box changes by less than 5 percent (SCALES WITH THE BOX). The radius changes by +36.4 percent and its share by −9.1 percent. The words of that rule were fixed after a pilot and carry no prediction.
5. **The growth slows.** The slope of the logarithms between neighboring boxes is 0.927, 0.874, 0.765 at L₁ = 0.3 and 0.773, 0.605, 0.485 at L₁ = 0.1. Four boxes do not pick a law. Of a power, a line and two saturating forms, a saturating one leaves the smallest residual at both values of L₁ (its limit at L₁ = 0.3, 25 to 44, is an extrapolation far past the boxes); a logarithm a + b ln L, which has no limit, fits better still at L₁ = 0.1 and worst at L₁ = 0.3. We claim no law and no limit.
