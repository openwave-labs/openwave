2. **The inertia gate (item 5).** The read: `ι_ab(x) = tr([T_a, M]ᵀ [T_b, M])`, with `M` the 3×3 order tensor of a stored census state and `T_a` the rotation generators acting by commutator, so that `Σ_a ι_aa = 2 Σ_{i<j} (λ_i − λ_j)²`. On all eleven stored endpoints (proton-analog, neutron-analog, lepton reference; δ 0.1 to 0.3; n 32 and 48) no direction in generator space is unbroken: the three eigenvalues of the shell-averaged tensor sit at 0.29 to 0.37 of its trace, and the integral follows the enclosed volume (0.78 to 0.83 of the vacuum density times the volume at δ 0.3). The gate is not flat, so N4 is not staged. Two limits. The stored states fill the pinned box: the outer-shell density is 0.86 of the vacuum value on the proton-analog and 0.74 on the neutron-analog, and 1.000 only on the pinned shell, so the box holds no vacuum exterior and the pre-registered word was withheld by its own clause. And the read takes N as the order tensor; under a spectral deviation from the vacuum (`N = M − Σ_k λ_k^vac v_k v_kᵀ` on the eigenvectors of `M`) the integral is not flat either, +73 percent from R = 14 to 22 on the proton-analog and thirteenfold on the neutron-analog. Code and data: [m5_38_inertia_gate.py](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/scripts/m5_38_inertia_gate.py), [m5_38_inertia_gate.json](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/data/m5_38_inertia_gate.json).

3. **The two clock checks (item 6).** The object is the fixed-clock electron of the OpenWave electron-ID instrument: the analytic biaxial hedgehog with the clock pinned at phase φ in the tilt plane, the charge density read as the divergence of the director's Mermin-Ho curvature, 16 phases, n 24, 32 and 48. (a) The charge centroid does not orbit: its transverse radius is 10⁻¹⁶ at every resolution, so the second moment is zero and the comparison with 3λ_C²/4 or λ_C²/4 is moot on this instrument. (b) The charge density is axially symmetric about the clock axis at every phase: every moving multipole is m = 0 and the transverse dipole is at round-off. The m = 0 part is not resolved. The lattice charge falls to zero at φ = π/2, but the degree of the unit director on a closed surface stays 1 up to π/2; the flux off the axis follows 4π cos φ and the rest runs on the frame's singular axis line, where the lattice sum loses it. The audit refuted a first reading of that fall as a moving monopole. In the twist plane the density is static exactly. The object is a seed with a pinned clock, not a relaxed spinning core. Code and data: [m5_38_clock_checks.py](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/scripts/m5_38_clock_checks.py), [m5_38_clock_checks.json](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/data/m5_38_clock_checks.json).
