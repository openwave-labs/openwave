# R22 results: the pinned pair returns the grounded-box Coulomb energy in the charge-odd channel; the core-seed test returns no separated branches and no compact endpoint; two of my own readings were refuted on the way

## TL;DR

1. **Form level.** The Faber identity holds exactly on our stencils. Our stack's normalization is twice the posted one, so the pair slope here is `64 pi (1 - delta)^4 / d` (48.27 / d at delta 0.3, the plan post's 24.14 in the author's units). It extends to a full frame field: the static quartic is THREE dual-Maxwell energies, one per eigenvector, each weighted by the squared product of the two gaps next to its axis.
2. **Pair gate.** A free box is a grounded wall for this functional, so the yardstick is the Dirichlet Green function of the cube, not `1 / d`. Against it the charge-odd energy is Coulomb within 1.7 to 3.0 percent on six points in two boxes, and 1.7 percent at h 0.75. The charge-even part is NOT shown to vanish: it is positive at the few-percent level in this pinned geometry.
3. **Core seeds.** Under one pinned uniaxial exterior the seeds end within 3.2 percent of each other in energy, and the object is not compact: it fills the box, and with the boundary released the unit hedgehog is lost. No separated branches and no compact endpoint on `(certified quartic + V4)` at n 32, L 48, h 1.5; a common converged branch is not established either, and two of the reads are resolution-limited.
4. **The instrument, again.** The first pass of item 3 was an optimizer stall on one stiff matrix entry. The audit found it, and also refuted a mechanism I had proposed for the fall. Both are recorded below as they happened.
5. **For Maciej.** Our route returns 0.32551859539640887 on the committed N 32 field. On our fields `omega_E` is 4 to 14 times the control's, and the number itself is not a continuum quantity (it scales as `h^(-3/2)` and with the envelope radius).

Every row names its validation script; each part was attacked by a fresh agent with its own code before this post (127 checks in four audits; 86 passed, and the 41 that failed are the corrections written into the tables below).

## 1. The form level (R22-0)

Equations. On `M_sp = delta I + (1 - delta) n n^T`: `F_ij = (1 - delta)^2 rho_ij [n]_x`, `rho_ij = n . (d_i n x d_j n)`. For a frame field `M_sp = R Lambda R^T` with body rates `W_i`:

```text
d_i M = R [w_i, Lambda] R^T
4 sum_{i<j} <F_ij, F_ij>_eta = 8 sum_a G_a^2 sum_{i<j} ((W_i x W_j)^a)^2,   G_a = prod_{b != a} (l_a - l_b)
(W_i x W_j)^a = rho_ij[e_a]        (Maurer-Cartan)
```

## 2. The pair gate (R22-2)

Setup. A unit director on the degenerate-pair vacuum, delta 0.3; pinned core balls of radius 2.5 carrying the exact single hedgehog; centers on x at `+/- d / 2`, the stereographic pole on z; seeds `w_1 w_2` (like, degree 2) and `w_1 conj(w_2)` (unlike, degree 0), no quotient map; every other cell free, the faces included; `U = E_pair - 2 E_ref` with the single pinned core in the same box. `k = 8 (1 - delta)^4 = 1.9208`.

The wall. The variation of `int abs(E_top)^2` leaves the boundary term `alpha . (E_top x nu)`, so at a free face `E_top` is normal: a grounded conductor. The prediction is `8 pi k G_D(d)` with the Dirichlet Green function of the cube (0.0893 at d 8, L 48, against 0.125). On the data the Neumann analog and free space are excluded (rms 0.74 and 1.46 against 0.10 to 0.14), and moving from L 48 to L 64 shifts U by `8 pi k Delta G_D` within 1 to 4 percent.

## 3. Inequivalent cores under one exterior (R22-1, the lepton paper's § 2.1 on the 4x4 field)

Setup. Certified quartic plus `V4` with targets `(-g, 1, delta, delta)`, g 8, delta 0.3, at `W1 x 25` and `W1`; one exterior, the hedgehog of the eigenvalue-1 director; five cores (radially melted, a wider melt, oblate, biaxial, and the eigenvalue 1 moved from the radial to the azimuthal axis); pinned exact exterior and free boundary. On a cubic lattice the permutations of a fixed core frame over x, y, z are symmetries of a spherical exterior, so they count as one seed.

So the lepton paper's § 2.4 result (three seeds, one branch, on the author's 3x3 five-component stack) is neither reproduced nor contradicted here: on the 4x4 field with `V4` there is no compact object to compare.

Two things I did not expect, stated as observations only. The interior shell means of every pinned field are BIAXIAL from r 6 to r 18: `(0.17, 0.45, 0.98)` at `W1 x 25` and `(0.20, 0.47, 0.96)` at `W1`, against the vacuum `(0.3, 0.3, 1)`, the same mean split at w and 25 w to 2 percent. That spectrum is held by the energy, not a slow residual: projecting the field back onto the uniaxial spectrum costs +43 to +47 (`W1 x 25`) and +11 (`W1`), nearly all of it curvature. It runs along `V4`'s valley: `tr S` and `tr S^2` stay at the vacuum's 1.6 and 1.18 (1.598 and 1.20), which is the quartic-flat direction of item 1. And the free boundary, which the R21 electron on the biaxial vacuum had seemed indifferent to, unwinds this one; the R21 statement was read at equal stall with the old polish and is open again.

The delta 0.89 side row is uninformative: the absolute `1e-3` gate was met after 6 iterations (the seed's gradient is a tenth of the delta 0.3 seed's). The n 64 box row was cut.
