# M5.39 Track A on Card C: results (the 33³ control did not pass)

**TL;DR.** The integrity pass, the independent evaluator, both ladders with a five-start census, the static ablation and the desk checks ran on OpenWave ([M5.39](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/main/openwave/xperiments/m5_liquid_crystal/research/tasks/m5_39_task_details.md), § 10 to § 17). Our 55³ inertia returns the printed 37.44 and the box rise from 55³ to 73³ is 1.31 percent, inside the window declared before the run. The 33³ control failed on the energy, so by the plan's own rule none of the comparison words (AGREES, CONVERGENT) is applied: the reads below are numbers. Three things we did not expect, all on the fields of this run: the force gate leaves the energy open at the 10⁻³ level, the inertia falls by about 4 percent from 55³ to zero spacing, and the archived seeds leave single-node record lumps that the gate does not see.

1. **R0, on the v3.4 archive (v3.5 and v3.6 checked by hash: the same 40 files identical, so nothing of the run changes with them).** Digests, packet checksums and the scorer's self-test pass. Of 19 printed values of the five hadronic scripts, 18 return at the printed digits; `rho_sextic.py` gives 1424.5 MeV at half strength, which is 1.42 GeV where the text prints 1.43. Two scripts of the STRING audit supplement do not run as shipped: `verify_hashes.py` needs `audit_v0.6/SHA256SUMS` and `screened_source_control.py` needs `audit_v0.6/archived_card_c/`, neither in the archive. The printed a = 2.0275 needs m_ρ = 775.26 MeV (775.3 gives 2.0273).

2. **R1, our evaluator.** Seven terms within 4 × 10⁻¹⁴ of the archived evaluation and the gradient within 9 × 10⁻¹⁵, at 33³ and 45³. Mirror check: under each coordinate reflection six terms return to round-off and the record quartic does not (its all-forward and all-backward stencils are not closed under one reflection); on a smooth state the defect falls with orders 0.3, 1.3, 1.8 across four grids, approaching the second.

3. **The 33³ control.** Printed: 76.05003, center (0.333, 0.376, 0.394). Our lowest gate-passing state of five starts: 76.05108, center (0.359, 0.359, 0.383), so it misses both. The `linked` route, continued past our caps, meets the gate at 14693 iterations with the printed center (0.332, 0.377, 0.394) at 76.05053. Continued with the optimizer's stop at a tenth of the gate it reaches 76.05036, still falling slowly (3 × 10⁻⁶ over its last 6000 iterations) and with its last iterate above the gate: 3.3 × 10⁻⁴ above the printed energy. The inertia is 34.32 on the states read (printed 34.33).

4. **What the census shows, in our runs.** Gate-passing states of one branch on one grid differ by up to 1.2 × 10⁻³ in energy (55³), against the 1.5 × 10⁻³ between the two printed 33³ branches. The split-center branch meets the gate on 45³ (two states), 1.3 to 6.4 × 10⁻⁴ above the isotropic-center ones, which spread by 3.3 × 10⁻⁴ themselves: unranked. On 77³ one of five starts met the gate in 8000 iterations, and that state carries one lump. Two instrument facts: a node left with a record eigenvalue near 0.96 far from the core is a local minimum (about 0.005 of energy each at h = 0.225, gradient a tenth of the gate; eight of them in our `smooth` 33³ run); and the gate is read on p, whose length grows to 3.9 in our relaxed states, which lowers the residual (rescaled to unit length the residuals rise, and none of the 18 states read crosses the gate).

5. **55³ and the couplings.** Inertia 37.438; the frozen rule on it gives e = 4.311, f_π = 91.30 MeV. Fitted tail 0.517 fm², g_πNN = 9.53 with M_N = 938.272 MeV, tail-implied axial coupling 0.927. The axial integral behind 0.904 is not computed: we did not find its definition on the card's fields.

6. **The two ladders pull opposite ways (our states).** Box, at h = 0.225: inertia 37.438, 37.929, 38.048 at half-boxes 6.075, 8.1, 10.125, a rise of 1.31 and then 0.31 percent (recorded before the run: about 1.1, with about 0.3 outside). Mesh, at half-box 6.075: 38.134, 37.438, 37.002, 36.685 on 45³, 55³, 65³, 77³, close to linear in h²; fits through three or four points extrapolate to 35.9 to 36.0. So our 55³ inertia sits about 4 percent above its zero-spacing value, more than twice the box rise measured up to half-box 10.125. The 77³ state is the one with a lump, which the inertia does not feel at this level.

7. **R3, the static ablation.** Energy change +0.0440, +0.0446, +0.0437 percent on 33³, 45³, 55³ (printed +0.0425, +0.0433, +0.0438); direct inertia part +2.1 to +3.0 × 10⁻⁴; relaxed change −2.9 to −4.7 × 10⁻⁴ (the 45³ parts lie outside the printed ranges); rms radius smaller by 1.6 to 1.8 × 10⁻⁴ fm. Our declared rule for this rung is not met: the ablated continuations did not reach the gate in 3000 iterations and were still falling, by about 3 × 10⁻⁴ of the change. One exact statement: on the lattice itself the cross term, −(2/G_time)⟨ΔP, Δ(a aᵀ)⟩ per edge, equals (1/G_time)(Δq)ᵀ Ā (Δq) with Ā the edge average of a aᵀ, to round-off, since aᵀq = 0 on every node.

8. **R4.** The moment bound and the first-order cranking statement check out as printed (199.5 MeV needs the nucleon average mass). The Finkelstein-Rubinstein filter, conditional on the winding field as the only sign source, reproduces the seven constraint sets of Manko, Manton and Wood (2007) for B = 2 to 8; B = 12 agrees with Krusch's 2003 table, three of whose third-column entries differ for B = 4, 6, 8 (for B = 4 and 6 the 2007 paper sides with our table).

9. **Three requests.** (a) The three `run.json` and the fields: the post of 13 November names the run records as attached and carries no file, and the v3.6 text places the fields on the Zenodo record as `CardC_payload_recovered.zip`, which none of its 26 versions lists. The route of the 33³ field is the missing input for the remaining 3.3 × 10⁻⁴. (b) The definitions behind the printed μ_V (3.004 against 3.008 from the rigid relation), r²_S and the axial integral. (c) Whether the calibration should wait for a mesh extrapolation, given item 6.

10. **v3.6 and the map.** Thank you for the contribution map. The five entries of the OpenWave lane carry the dates of the OpenWave roadmap. The constrained tilt reduction on a relaxed winding-one texture is noted as a request; it gets its own plan (which texture, which reduction, what is scored) before any run.
